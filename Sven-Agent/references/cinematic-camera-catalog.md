## 📸 § 1. THE 8 PSYCHOLOGICAL SHOT SCALES (CỠ CẢNH & TÂM LÝ HỌC)

Every shot scale exists to deliver a precise **degree of psychological and emotional pressure**:

---

### 1. Extreme Close-Up (ECU / Đặc Tả Vi Mô)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `100mm Macro` · `f/2.8` to `f/4.0` (Razor-thin Depth of Field).
* **Framing**: Hyper-magnified detail: eye pupil, scar ridge, bead of cold sweat, trembling fingertip, burning candle wick, cracking wax.
* **Psychological Function**: **Peak Visceral Truth / Lie Exposure.** Strips away all social masks; exposes involuntary biological micro-reactions that cannot be suppressed or faked.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 4 (Ego Death / Anagnorisis)** or **Beat 1 (Cliffhanger Freeze)**.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Revealing pupil dilation when confronted with an undeniable truth.
  - Micro-tremors in fingers holding a weapon, contract, or poison vial.
  - Extreme tactile friction (quill snapping, tear cutting through soot on cheek).
* **Strict Prohibitions**:
  - NEVER use for continuous multi-sentence spoken dialogue (causes lip-sync facial warping).
  - NEVER execute rapid camera pans in ECU (causes extreme motion blur artifacts).
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:07–00:10] Shot Extreme Close-Up | 100mm Macro f/2.8 | Static locked focus on [Subject]'s dark pupil dilating in paralyzed shock as heat embers reflect across the cornea...`

---

### 2. Close-Up (CU / Cận Cảnh Gương Mặt)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `85mm Portrait Prime` · `f/1.8` to `f/2.8`.
* **Framing**: Full human face from chin to the crown of the head.
* **Psychological Function**: **Direct Emotional Intimacy & Raw Vulnerability.** Forces the audience into direct empathetic synchronization with the character's suffering, fury, or predatory resolve.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 4 (Climax Peak)**, sudden revelation of mortal stakes, confession moments.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Delivering the single most devastating psychological truth of the scene.
  - Capturing jaw muscle knots, flare of nostrils, and tears welling up.
  - Intimate 1-on-1 emotional climaxes.
* **Strict Prohibitions**:
  - Do NOT include complex multi-limb body movements in CU (frame is too tight).
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:03–00:07] Shot Low-Angle Close-Up | 85mm f/1.8 | Slow forward push-in tightening on [Subject]'s clenched jaw and unblinking glare catching 2700K brazier sidelight...`

---

### 3. Choker Shot (Cận Cảnh Ngột Ngạt)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `85mm` · `f/2.0`.
* **Framing**: Tight cut from just below the lower lip/chin to the upper forehead hairline.
* **Psychological Function**: **Claustrophobic Deadlock & Breathless Entrapment.** Eliminates the surrounding room entirely; traps characters in an inescapable psychological corner.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 3.3 (Exits Sealed)** and **Beat 4.3 (All Hope Is Lost)**.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Breathless ultimatums where no compromise exists.
  - Interrogation standoffs where the subject is physically backed against a wall.
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:07–00:10] Shot Choker Standoff | 85mm f/2.0 | Static lock holding breathless deadlock between [Subject A] and [Subject B] inches apart...`

---

### 4. Medium Close-Up (MCU / Trung Cận Đối Thoại)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `50mm–85mm` · `f/2.0` to `f/2.8`.
* **Framing**: From mid-chest up to the top of the head.
* **Psychological Function**: **Workhorse of Cinematic Dialogue (60% Coverage).** Perfect balance between nuanced facial biomechanics and chest/shoulder tension.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 1 (Disruptive Hook)** and **Beat 3 (Escalating Ideological Clash)**.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Primary ping-pong dialogue confrontations.
  - Capturing rapid breathing, posture stiffening, and throat swallows while delivering lines.
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:00–00:03] Shot Medium Close-Up | 50mm f/2.0 | Eye-level static focus on [Subject] delivering ultimatum with rising chest tension...`

---

### 5. Medium Shot (MS / Trung Cảnh Thao Tác)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `50mm Standard Prime` · `f/2.8`.
* **Framing**: From the waist up. Human-eye natural perspective.
* **Psychological Function**: **Physical Interaction & Prop Manipulation.** Balances character identity with active physical manipulation of the environment.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 2 (Setup)** and **Beat 3 (Escalating Drama)**.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Table slamming, drawing weapons from belts, signing contracts, sliding ledgers, pouring tea.
  - Upper-body physical gestures, grabbing collars, presenting evidence.
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:03–00:07] Shot Medium Shot | 50mm f/2.8 | Lateral tracking reframe as [Subject] forcefully slides the bronze deed across the mahogany table plane...`

---

### 6. Cowboy Shot (MLS / Trung Toàn Nghênh Chiến)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `35mm–50mm` · `f/2.8` to `f/4.0`.
* **Framing**: From mid-thigh up (Western Standoff heritage).
* **Psychological Function**: **Action Readiness & Latent Combat Authority.** Directs visual focus to hands hovering near holsters, sword hilts, keys, or concealed evidence.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 1 (Combat Hook)** and **Beat 3 (Pre-Standoff Tension)**.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Standoffs across a boardroom or grand hall.
  - Establishing who is prepared to draw blood or unleash an ultimatum.
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:00–00:03] Shot Low-Angle Cowboy Shot | 35mm f/2.8 | Slow push-in on [Subject] standing rigid with right hand hovering inches above the iron dagger hilt...`

---

### 7. Two-Shot / Wide (WS / Toàn Cảnh Đối Đầu 1-1)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `35mm–50mm` · `f/4.0`.
* **Framing**: Both characters in full or 3/4 body with the physical space/barrier between them.
* **Psychological Function**: **Power Balance & Spatial Geography.** Defines the territorial tension, distance (1.5m–3m), and 1-on-1 power standoff.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 1 (Opening Hook)**, **Beat 3 (Negotiation Setup)**, **Beat 6 (Reconciliation)**.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Setting up tabletop negotiations, throne room confrontations, or alleyway encounters.
  - Ensuring Continuous Co-Presence without cutting.
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:00–00:03] Shot Eye-Level Medium Two-Shot | 50mm f/1.4 | Slow forward push-in: [Subject A] at frame-right faces [Subject B] at frame-left across the wooden table...`

---

### 8. Extreme Wide Shot (EWS/ELS / Đại Cảnh Định Mệnh)
* **Lens & Aperture**: `16mm–24mm Ultra-Wide` · `f/5.6` to `f/8.0` (Deep Depth of Field).
* **Framing**: Vast monumental terrain, architecture, or cosmic backdrop; human subjects appear microscopic.
* **Psychological Function**: **Cosmic / Karmic Omniscience & Human Solitude.** Illustrates human helplessness, fatalism, or spiritual transcendence before empire or nature.
* **Arc Application**: **Beat 2 (World Setup)** or **Beat 6 (Cosmic Echo)**.
* **Best Used For**:
  - Lone traveler walking across ruined battlefields, frozen tundras, or grand cathedral halls.
  - Closing maxim shots showing the transformed world.
* **Prompt Syntax**:
  `△ [00:07–00:10] Shot Extreme Wide Shot | 16mm f/8.0 | Static locked crane shot capturing the single tiny silhouette of [Subject] standing beneath towering gothic arches...`

### 4.1. THE 360-DEGREE ORBIT DIRECTORIAL & PROMPT PROTOCOL (Quy Chuẩn Cú Xoay 360°)

```
                                    [Top-Down View of 360° Orbit]
                                            00:05 (Back 180°)
                                                  ▲
                                                  │
                 00:03 (Right 90°)  ◄──── [SUBJECT (X:50%, Y:50%)] ────►  00:07 (Left 270°)
                                                  │
                                                  ▼
                                            00:00 / 00:10
                                          (Front Face 0°/360°)
                                    [Dialogue Delivery & Freeze Hold]
```

When executing a 360-degree continuous orbital take in a 10s AI video prompt:

1. **Rotational Velocity Formula**:
   $$\mathbf{Angular\ Velocity} = \frac{360^\circ}{10\text{ seconds}} = \mathbf{36^\circ / \text{second}}$$
   - **Beat 1 `[00:00–00:03]`**: Arc $0^\circ \to 90^\circ$ (Front face $\to$ Right shoulder).
   - **Beat 2 `[00:03–00:07]`**: Arc $90^\circ \to 270^\circ$ (Right shoulder $\to$ Full back $\to$ Left shoulder, revealing the background encirclement/threat).
   - **Beat 3 `[00:07–00:10]`**: Arc $270^\circ \to 360^\circ$ (Left shoulder $\to$ Front face complete lock).

2. **Subject Central Axis Lock**:
   - The subject MUST serve as the static rotational center anchored at screen coordinates $(X:50\%, Y:50\%)$.
   - Explicit prompt phrase: `[Subject Name] remains anchored at center frame throughout the entire 360-degree rotation.`

3. **Lip-Sync & Dialogue Synchronization Rule in 360° Orbit**:
   - **CRITICAL**: Spoken dialogue lines MUST NEVER be placed during the fast-rotating back phase ($00:00–00:07$).
   - Spoken dialogue is strictly synchronized to the **final static lock phase `[00:07–00:10]`** when the camera completes the circle back to the front face and enters the **0.5s Zero-Velocity Freeze Hold**.

## ⚡ § 5. TRANSITIONS: IN-CAMERA (1 TAKE) VS. TIMELINE EDITING CUTS

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               THE MASTER TRANSITION TAXONOMY                                       |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| GROUP 1: IN-CAMERA TRANSITIONS (Trong 1 Cú Máy 10s — Zero Cut)                                     |
| • 1. The Kinetic Prop Bridge: Camera tracks moving prop (thrown scepter/sliding coin) from A to B. |
| • 2. Steadicam Arc Pivot: Sweeps semi-circular arc (90°–180°) from behind A to frame B directly.   |
| • 3. Optical Rack Focus: Static camera shifts focus from foreground A to background B.             |
| • 4. Vertical Elevation Tilt: Sweeps vertically from elevated dais (A) down to floor level (B).     |
| • 5. Continuous Lateral Reframe: Glides horizontally across table, dropping A and centering B.     |
| • 6. Foreground Occlusion Pass: A pillar or dark shoulder wipes across frame, revealing new focal. |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| GROUP 2: TIMELINE EDITING CUTS (Hậu Kỳ Dựng Phim / Ghép Cues)                                      |
| • 1. Shot-Reverse-Shot: Alternates between opposing angles/OTS for ping-pong dialogue battles.     |
| • 2. Reaction Shot: Cuts away from speaker to capture the silent, visceral reaction of listener.   |
| • 3. Graphic Match Cut: Connects two scenes via identical geometric silhouette (circle to circle). |
| • 4. Kinetic Match Cut: Connects two scenes via matching physical velocity vectors (fall to fall). |
| • 5. Cutaway (Insert Cut): Brief 1s insert of critical physical detail (clock, poison, dripping blood)
| • 6. Smash Cut: Violent jarring cut from high-decibel chaos to dead silence (or vice versa).       |
| • 7. Intercutting (Cross-Cutting): Alternates between two simultaneous events in different spaces. |
| • 8. J-Cut & L-Cut: Sound leads image (J-Cut) or sound lingers across cut into next scene (L-Cut). |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 5.1. Deep Dive: In-Camera Transitions (Zero-Cut 10s Takes)

1. **The Kinetic Prop Bridge (Tracking Pan via Prop)**:
   - *Mechanics*: Character A hurls, slides, or drops a physical object; the camera latches onto the prop's momentum, panning across the floor/table until the prop strikes Character B's boots or hands.
   - *Use Case*: Ideological challenges, delivering surrender deeds, throwing gauntlets.
   - *Prompt Syntax*: `Kaelen hurls the bronze signet across the flagstones; camera executes a fluid tracking pan following the spinning bronze disc as it sparks against stone and clatters into Tarik's iron boots.`

2. **Steadicam Arc Pivot (90°–180° Orbital Glide)**:
   - *Mechanics*: Camera begins behind Character A's dark shoulder, glides smoothly in a semi-circular arc around the table, descending to frame Character B's face directly.
   - *Use Case*: Shifting power dynamics; revealing an opponent's reaction without cutting.
   - *Prompt Syntax*: `Steadicam executes a descending 120-degree orbital arc pivot around Kaelen's shoulder, sweeping across the tabletop to reframe tightly on Tarik rising from shadow.`

3. **Optical Rack Focus (Focus Pull)**:
   - *Mechanics*: Camera remains 100% static on a shallow lens (`85mm f/1.4`). Shifts optical focal plane from Subject A's trembling knuckles in the foreground to Subject B's cold smile in the background.
   - *Use Case*: Connecting the weapon of cause to the person of effect; betrayal reveals.
   - *Prompt Syntax*: `Static locked camera; optical rack focus shifts sharpness from the leaking poison vial on the foreground table to the Duchess's unblinking cold gaze in the blurred background.`

4. **Vertical Elevation Tilt (Dais to Floor)**:
   - *Mechanics*: Camera tilts smoothly down a vertical axis (e.g. from an empress on a high throne down the marble stairs to a kneeling general).
   - *Use Case*: Visualizing hierarchical oppression and royal judgment.
   - *Prompt Syntax*: `Low-angle vertical tilt descends smoothly from the Queen seated on the high golden throne down the marble steps to land tightly on the kneeling General bound in chains.`

### 5.2. Deep Dive: Timeline Editing Cuts & Match Cut Execution Protocol

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         GRAPHIC & KINETIC MATCH CUT 4-POINT CONTRACT                               |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Coordinate Parity  : Terminal frame of Cue A and Opening frame of Cue B must lock the focal     |
|                         object at the exact same screen coordinates (e.g., X:50%, Y:50%).          |
| 2. Geometry Alignment : Matching geometric silhouettes (Circle ➔ Circle / Triangle ➔ Triangle).   |
| 3. Kinetic Velocity   : Velocity vectors must maintain directional continuity (Down ➔ Down).       |
| 4. Chromatic Harmony  : Shared color temperature bridge (e.g., 2200K Red Iron ➔ Crimson Wax Seal). |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Graphic Match Cut (Cắt Đồng Dạng Hình Khối)**:
   - *Mechanics*: The concluding frame of Clip A contains a prominent geometric shape at screen coordinates $(X, Y)$; the opening frame of Clip B opens on an identical geometric shape at the exact same coordinates.
   - *Archetypal Examples*:
     - Circular glowing iron brand (Clip A) $\longrightarrow$ Circular red wax seal (Clip B).
     - Spinning roulette wheel (Clip A) $\longrightarrow$ Spinning carriage wheel in mud (Clip B).
     - Circular pupil dilating (Clip A) $\longrightarrow$ Solar eclipse in desert sky (Clip B).

2. **Kinetic Match Cut (Cắt Đồng Dạng Động Lực Học)**:
   - *Mechanics*: An action begins with high velocity in Clip A (e.g. sword swinging downward from top-right to bottom-left); Clip B opens with an identical downward velocity vector (e.g. a butcher's cleaver slamming down on a wooden block).
   - *Use Case*: Generational parallels, flashes of trauma, poetic causality across time.

3. **Reaction Shot (Cú Máy Bắt Phản Ứng)**:
   - *Mechanics*: Speaker delivers a crushing verbal blow in Cue 1; timeline immediately cuts to Cue 2 framing the silent listener's face as the emotional impact lands (tears, pupil dilation, trembling lip).
   - *Use Case*: Emotional climaxes where how the truth feels matters more than how it was said.

4. **Smash Cut (Cắt Giật Đột Ngột)**:
   - *Mechanics*: Cut abruptly from an ear-shattering explosion or screaming crowd (100dB, chaotic camera) to an absolute dead-silent empty room (0dB, locked static camera).
   - *Use Case*: Heart attacks, waking from nightmares, sudden death, sudden moral disillusionment.

5. **J-Cut & L-Cut (Âm Thanh Dẫn Đường)**:
   - *J-Cut*: The diegetic dialogue or sound of Scene B begins playing 1.5 seconds BEFORE the video cuts from Scene A.
   - *L-Cut*: The dialogue or emotional weeping from Scene A continues echoing for 2 seconds AFTER the video has already transitioned into Scene B.
