# CINEMATIC EMOTION, MICRO-BIOMECHANICS & TACTILE PHYSICS ENCYCLOPEDIA

This master encyclopedia catalogs the complete translation matrix for the **6 Universal Emotional Archetypes**, **Continuous Co-Presence Somatics**, **Domain-Specific Tactile Physics**, and the **4-in-1 Parenthetical Dialogue Directing Syntax** for AI Video Prompts (SVEN / Dialogue-Agent) and Still Image Prompts.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **THE ANTI-ABSTRACT EMOTION LAW:**
> AI Video & Image generators (Kling, Runway Gen-3/4, Sora, Midjourney, Flux) **CANNOT render abstract emotion words** (❌ "angry", "sad", "joyful", "terrified", "arrogant").
> Abstract words produce stiff, dead mannequin faces and static poses.
> Every emotional state MUST be translated into **3 Observable Physical Components**:
> 1. **Vocal Acoustic & Delivery Tone**: Specific timbre, pitch, breathiness, and cadence of speech.
> 2. **Facial Micro-Biomechanics**: Eyebrow angle, pupil dilation, nostril flare, lip tension, jaw knots, neck tendons, cheek flushing, teardrop trail.
> 3. **Body Physicality & Somatic Action**: Hand contact, knuckle blanching, spine posture, shoulder drop, breathing depth, foot bracing.

## 🔗 § 2. CONTINUOUS CO-PRESENCE & DUAL-ACTION SOMATICS

Generative video models suffer from **Subject Amnesia** if characters are described in isolation. To maintain absolute character permanence and dramatic realism across all 10 seconds:

1. **Both Characters Anchored Continuously**: Throughout the entire shot, both characters must be anchored in space (Frame-Left vs. Frame-Right).
2. **Every Second is Bidirectional (Dual-Action Rule)**:

$$\mathbf{Subject\ A\ Initiates\ Physical\ Action}\ \longleftrightarrow\ \mathbf{Subject\ B\ Somatic\ Reaction\ Simultaneously}$$

* **Canonical Directorial Example**:
  `△ [00:03–00:07]: Kaelen Song at frame-right forcefully slides the bronze deed across the wood, gouging a white scratch into the varnish; directly opposite at frame-left, Tarik Al-Mansur clamps his iron-gauntleted palm over the parchment the instant it strikes his cuffs, chest heaving in unblinking defiance.`

## ⏱️ § 5. MAXIMUM DIALOGUE DENSITY & HARD-CAP $\le 160$ CHARS

To maximize storytelling richness, philosophical depth, and natural lip-sync synchronization in AI video models (Kling, Runway, Sora, Veo):

1. **Optimal Sweet Spot (~140–160 English Characters / ~20–25 Words)**:
   - Dialogue lines should ALWAYS strive to hit the **~140–160 character range** to maximize dramatic substance and world-building.
   - Every line must be a complete, multi-layered dramatic statement combining **Contextual Bite + Moral Conflict + Devastating Consequence**.
2. **Absolute Ban on Fragmented / Short Lines**:
   - Strictly FORBIDDEN to write brief, shallow, or monosyllabic lines (e.g. "Ten years", "Yes", "Stop it", < 50 chars). Choppy fragments lack meaning, feel uncinematic, and waste the precious 10s narrative capacity.
3. **Hard-Cap Ceiling**:
   - Total spoken dialogue per 10-second clip MUST NOT exceed **160 English characters** to prevent AI lip-sync warping and preserve the 0.5s terminal freeze hold.
