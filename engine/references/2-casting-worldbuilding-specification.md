     - Full cross-faction web (Betrayal, Secret Guardian, Legal Enforcer, Healer).

## 🌊 THE LIVING CONTINUUM: BASELINE PRESENCE & DYNAMIC FLUIDITY

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ANTI-FRAGMENTATION & ANTI-MECHANICAL CAUSALITY MANDATE (CHỐNG CỤC BỘ & CHỐNG MÁY MÓC NHÂN QUẢ)**:
> Characters are NOT static ID cards, nor are they deterministic logic calculators:
> 1. **No Localized Fragmentation (Tuyệt đối không cục bộ)**: Never reduce a character to an isolated checklist of appearance metrics (height, weight, pristine garments) paired with detached, per-shot gesture stickers (e.g. "Shot 1 clenches jaw, Shot 2 grinds teeth, Shot 3 sweats"). Characters must move as a seamless, living whole.
> 2. **No Mechanical Causality Traps (Không gò ép nhân quả một chiều)**: Do not force characters into robotic cause-and-effect melodramas ("Scene 1 insulted -> Scene 2 boils with rage -> Scene 3 exacts revenge"). Real humans possess rich, unpredictable, and multi-layered psychological impulses: they laugh bitterly at catastrophe, become suddenly numb under mortal shock, make mundane deflections (averting gaze, adjusting collar, trailing off), or absorb devastating strikes with profound, unhurried stillness.
> 3. **The Two Core State Dimensions**:
>    - **Baseline Presence (Trạng Thái Ban Đầu — Khí Chất & Nhịp Sống Tự Nhiên)**: Who they are when alone in an unobserved room. Their inherent life tempo, natural spatial occupation, and default atmospheric aura.
>    - **Dynamic Fluidity (Trạng Thái Động — Dòng Chảy Hữu Cơ & Thích Ứng Khoảnh Khắc)**: How their natural rhythm flexes, deflects, and adapts in the living moment when encountering another human being.

# Character Relationships — Triadic Cross-Axis & Subtext Wiring

## The Core Directorial Principle

Characters do not exist in isolation. They exist in **relationship** — and the friction of those relationships, their secrets, debts, unspoken vows, and clashing loyalties generate the entire kinetic energy of cinematic drama. Plot is what happens; relationship is why the audience weeps, debates, and hits replay.

In the Sven Cinematic Directorial Framework, all relationships are architected around the **Triadic Cross-Axis**:

```

                       └────────────┬────────────┘
                                    ▲

                       │   A2: MAIN ANTAGONIST   │

                                    │
               ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐

   ┌───────────────────────┐                 ┌───────────────────────┐
```

## 🏛️ THE TRIADIC CROSS-AXIS: A1 ⇄ B ⇄ A2 (DUAL-RELATIONSHIP MANDATE)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NO CHARACTER SILOS ALLOWED**:
> A protagonist's ally who has zero connection to the antagonist creates a flat, predictable story. 
> Every Tier B satellite (`B1, B2...`) must possess **a double tether**:
> - A personal/emotional/economic link to **A1**.
> - A secret/structural/legal leverage point with **A2**.

> This dual relationship creates the **voltage drop** that powers the story's Plot Twists (`TW-01` to `TW-20`).

### 1. The Jealous Foil Tether (A1 ↔ B-01 ↔ A2)
* **To A1**: Outward loyalty as second-in-command; inward toxic envy of A1's natural charisma.
* **To A2**: Sells A1's secrets for promised executive power.
* **Dramatic Spark**: When A1 faces peril, B-01 hesitates, realizing too late that A2 regards them as disposable vermin.

### 2. The Hostage/Blackmail Tether (A1 ↔ B-02 ↔ A2)
* **To A1**: Devoted love and family loyalty.
* **To A2**: Economic or legal hostage held by A2's extortion.
* **Dramatic Spark**: The gut-wrenching moment A1 discovers the person they love most was the one who handed over the key to the enemy.

### 3. The Secret Guardian Tether (A1 ↔ B-05 ↔ A2)
* **To A2**: Harsh enforcer executing orders to preserve cover.
* **To A1**: Unpayable blood debt to A1's ancestors; sabotages A2's traps from within.
* **Dramatic Spark**: A1 believes they are about to be destroyed, but the enforcer strikes A2's weapon aside or drops the true key.

### 4. The Moral Bridge Tether (A1 ↔ B-09/B-11 ↔ A2)
* **To A1**: Adopted child, beloved partner, or shared apprentice.
* **To A2**: Biological kin, estranged child, or vital legacy.
* **Dramatic Spark**: Stepping between A1 and A2 at the climactic confrontation, forcing A2 to choose between pride and blood.

## THE 3-LAYER RELATIONSHIP DEPTH MODEL

Always define all 3 layers before scripting any scene between two characters:

* **Layer 1 — The Surface Label**: What society calls them. (Partner, Rival, Landlord, Bodyguard, Cousin).
* **Layer 2 — The Real Dynamic**: What the relationship actually is under pressure. (The subordinate who holds all real authority; the bitter debtor who secretly yearns for affection).
* **Layer 3 — The Wound Connection**: The shared or interlocking past trauma connecting them. (Both survived the same famine; one ran away while the other stayed behind to bury the dead).

## 🎭 CLASSIC DRAMATIC RELATIONSHIP PATTERNS

### TYPE 1: THE ESTRANGED BOND (Mối Dây Xa Cách Hóa Đá)
* **Definition**: Two characters once intimately close who grew distant over years of calcified silence.
* **Dramatic Fuel**: First contact after a decade; the acute pain of being known by someone who remembers your innocent self.
* **Directorial Key**: Dialogue is sparse. Tension lives in physical avoidance — never meeting eyes, busying hands with tea cups or ledgers.

### TYPE 2: THE COMFORTABLE TRAP (Cái Bẫy Ấm Êm)
* **Definition**: A relationship that was once supportive but has become stagnant and suffocating.
* **Dramatic Fuel**: One party attempts to grow or break out, and the other fights to preserve the comfortable status quo.
* **Directorial Key**: The "trapping" character is not evil; they are fighting from genuine fear of loneliness.

### TYPE 3: THE UNEXPECTED ALLIANCE (Liên Minh Bất Ngờ)
* **Definition**: Two characters from completely hostile or dissimilar backgrounds who find rare mutual understanding.
* **Dramatic Fuel**: Discovering that an ostensible enemy sees your soul more clearly than your own blood family.
* **Directorial Key**: Disarming honesty spoken in casual, unguarded moments.

### TYPE 4: THE INHERITED WOUND (Vết Thương Truyền Kiếp)
* **Definition**: Two characters carrying a trauma handed down by a previous generation (ancestral feud, family debt).
* **Dramatic Fuel**: The electrifying realization: *"We are killing each other over a lie our fathers invented."*
* **Directorial Key**: The climax requires breaking the cycle through radical forgiveness, not vengeance.

### 2. The Dual-Value Optical Contrast Inversion Law (Luật Tương Phản Đối Ứng Độ Sáng)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **THE OPTICAL VALUE CONTRAST INVERSION LAW**:
> Diffusion and latent models suffer from severe edge-bleed and chromatic hallucination when subject value matches backdrop value. Therefore, studio backdrops MUST strictly invert the brightness value of the character's baseline wardrobe:
> - **Dark Wardrobe (Black Suit, Charcoal Leather, Dark Wool)** ➔ **SOLID PURE WHITE STUDIO BACKDROP (`solid pure white studio background`)**.
>   *Rationale:* Pure white renders every shoulder seam, arm silhouette, and lapel fold with razor-sharp contrast, completely eliminating "Black-on-Black" clipping or silhouette vanishing.
> - **Light Wardrobe (White Dress, Ivory Tunic, Cream Linen)** ➔ **SOLID DARK NEUTRAL GREY STUDIO BACKDROP (`solid dark neutral grey studio background`)**.
>   *Rationale:* Dark neutral grey renders crisp luminous highlights on white fabric edges and fine linen weaves, completely eliminating "White-on-White" edge blowout.
> - **STRICT BAN ON ENVIRONMENTAL SCENERY**:
>   *Directorial Mandate:* Strictly zero background scenery, zero environmental elements, zero streets, zero trees, zero rooms, zero furniture. Backgrounds must be 100% solid, flat, seamless studio infinity cycloramas. Any environmental clutter in a model sheet leaks uncontrollably into downstream video generation prompts.

## 🏛️ PART IV: THE LIVING CONTINUUM: BASELINE PRESENCE & DYNAMIC FLUIDITY

﻿# Character State Dynamics — Baseline Presence & Dynamic Fluidity

## The Directorial Philosophy: Beyond Fragmentation & Mechanical Causality

In conventional screenwriting and video prompt engineering, characters suffer from two fatal pitfalls:
1. **Localized Fragmentation (Bệnh Cục Bộ Hóa)**: The character is treated as an isolated "ID card" in pre-production (height, hair, clothes) and then arbitrarily assigned random, mechanical micro-gestures per shot (clenching fists, flaring nostrils, sweating). These gestures feel like detached stickers slapped onto a mannequin rather than the lived expression of a human soul.
2. **Mechanical Causality Traps (Bệnh Nhân Quả Cơ Học)**: The character is treated like a deterministic calculator: *"Because event A happened in Scene 1, they MUST compress anger in Scene 2, and explode in Scene 3."* This produces robotic, melodramatic caricatures. Authentic human psychology is far richer, messier, and more unpredictable: a person in mortal crisis may feel sudden emotional numbness, make a mundane comment about the weather, chuckle bitterly at the absurdity of their situation, or fall into unhurried silence.

To achieve Hollywood-grade realism and emotional resonance, characters must be designed and directed through **The Living Continuum**:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                 THE LIVING CONTINUUM FRAMEWORK                                   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. BASELINE PRESENCE (Trạng Thái Ban Đầu — Khí Chất & Nhịp Sống Tự Nhiên)                        │
│    • Who they are when alone in an unobserved room.                                              │
│    • Inherent biological tempo, natural spatial occupation, and default atmospheric aura.       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. DYNAMIC FLUIDITY (Trạng Thái Động — Dòng Chảy Hữu Cơ & Thích Ứng Khoảnh Khắc)                 │
│    • How their natural rhythm flexes and adapts when reality and other humans intrude.           │
│    • Living in the moment: hesitation, mundane deflections, contradictory feelings, pauses.      │
│    • Seamless holistic continuity rather than per-shot fragmented reaction ticks.                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🏛️ PART 1: BASELINE PRESENCE (TRẠNG THÁI BAN ĐẦU)

Baseline Presence is **not** a catalog of physical measurements. It is the **gravitational aura** the audience feels within two seconds of seeing the character on screen.

### 1. Inherent Life Tempo (Nhịp Thở Cuộc Đời)
Every human being moves through the world at a distinct internal frequency:
* **The Unhurried Stoic**: Slow, deliberate movements; pauses before answering; energy remains calm and low-frequency regardless of background chaos.
* **The Restless Sentinel**: Quick darting glances, fingers tapping lightly against thighs, speaking with clipped efficiency, constantly scanning perimeter sightlines.
* **The Weary Laborer**: Heavy steps, shoulders carrying a chronic bone-deep exhaustion, economy of motion (refuses to expend unnecessary energy).
* **The Agile Chameleon**: Light on their feet, loose posture, ready to shift tone and stance at a moment's notice.

### 2. Natural Spatial Occupation (Cách Chiếm Lĩnh Không Gian)
How does the character inhabit physical reality before drama arrives?
* *Peripheral Recoil*: Instinctively sits with back to the wall, tucks limbs inward, minimizing their silhouette to avoid drawing attention.
* *Sovereign Expansiveness*: Slumps casually across the center of a bench, elbows spread wide, claiming physical territory without asking permission.
* *Quiet Center of Gravity*: Stands dead-center, perfectly balanced on both feet, neither retreating nor imposing—a grounded boulder in running water.

### 3. Default Atmospheric Aura (Nhiệt Độ Năng Lượng Mặc Định)
* Is their natural aura **Warm & Approachable**, **Chilly & Aloof**, **Dry & Sardonic**, or **Fierce & Coiled**?
* This aura is established *before* any plot conflict occurs, grounding the character as a living entity rather than a dramatic pawn.

## 🌊 PART 2: DYNAMIC FLUIDITY (TRẠNG THÁI ĐỘNG)

Dynamic State is **not** a list of isolated acting ticks. It is **how the character's baseline presence bends, absorbs, or deflects the impact of another human being in real time**.

### 1. Living in the Moment & Mundane Deflections (Sự Sống Trong Từng Tích Tắc)
Real people rarely deliver melodrama under stress; they exhibit authentic, mundane coping mechanisms:
* **The Averting Gaze**: When hit with a painful truth, they don't grimace dramatically—they look out the window at the rain, trace a knot in the wooden table, or adjust their collar.
* **The Trail-Off**: A character speaks with sudden passion, then stops mid-thought, realizing the futility of their words, letting silence finish the sentence.
* **The Bitter Chuckle**: Finding grim humor in an impossible bind rather than shouting or sobbing.
* **The Delayed Reaction**: Absorbing a devastating line with complete, impassive stillness for three seconds before a slight, almost imperceptible softening of the eyes reveals the strike landed.

### 2. Multi-Layered Emotional Ambivalence (Cảm Xúc Đa Tầng, Phi Một Chiều)
Emotions are never singular:
* A confrontation can evoke **anger mixed with lingering affection**.
* A betrayal can provoke **disgust paired with a quiet, sickening pity**.
* Facing ruin can bring **panic intertwined with an unexpected, liberating relief**.
* *Directional Rule*: Never direct an actor or prompt an AI model with a single flat emotion (`"He is angry"`). Always direct the friction between two competing impulses: `"A weary cynicism fighting down a sudden surge of defensive pride."`

### 3. Relational Elasticity & Resonance (Nhịp Điệu Co Giãn Tương Tác)
Dynamic state exists strictly in the **relational space between two bodies**:
* **Expansion vs. Contraction**: When the antagonist steps forward aggressively, the protagonist doesn't just "pose"; their personal space contracts—breathing slows, peripheral vision narrows, feet anchor deeper into the floorboards.
* **Vocal & Physical Synchrony / Disconnection**: One character speaks rapidly, trying to overwhelm; the other responds in a slow, hushed whisper, forcing the room's entire acoustic tension down to their level.

## 📋 SCREENPLAY & PROMPT SPECIFICATION RULES

When writing scenes and prompts:
1. **Never write static mannequin descriptions** in scene action lines. Anchor the subject via their **Baseline Rhythm** (e.g., `Finn moves with the heavy, unhurried stride of a veteran rigger`).
2. **Anchor dynamic states through physical fluidity**, not isolated medical symptoms (e.g., instead of `his veins pulse with rage`, write: `he lets out a low, dry breath, eyes tracing the dust motes between them, refusing to meet Eleazar's gaze`).
3. **Allow the dramatic pause (Beat)**: Dramatic truth lives in the unsaid seconds between lines, where a character's dynamic state visibly recalibrates without a single spoken syllable.

## 📌 6. SUMMARY OF OPERATIONAL GOLDEN RULES

1. **Setting Bible is Pre-Production Only:** It establishes characters, props, environments, diurnal lighting, master script spine, and scene roadmaps. NEVER generate clip-level rendering prompts in `setting.html`.
2. **English First Spoken Dialogue:** In the `.dialogue-breakdown-box`, the primary quote (`.dia-en-quote`) must be in English to ensure audio-lip synchronization in AI video pipelines. Vietnamese is used for subtitles (`.dia-vn-dub`) and acting notes (`.dia-parenthetical-text`).
3. **Density Cap (14–18 words):** Dialogue must fit within the strict 10s audio duration limit.
4. **Zero Continuity Gaps:** Diurnal progression (time & Kelvin) and kinetic vectors must connect seamlessly across all 48 scenes and between episodes.
5. **Mandatory 12-Hour AM/PM Time Notation:** Every timestamp across Section 1, Section 1B, Section 1C, and all 48 scene cards in Section 5 MUST explicitly state **AM** or **PM** (e.g., `05:30 PM`, `10:30 PM`, `01:00 AM`, `04:30 AM`, `05:00 AM`, `06:30 AM`). Ambiguous 24-hour military numbers without AM/PM (e.g. `01:00` or `17:30`) are strictly prohibited.

## 🔄 § 6. MASTER TROPE LIBRARY (18 NARRATIVE ENGINES)

1. **Contract Marriage**: Two rivals enter a legal marriage for money/power; physical cohabitation sparks authentic mutual passion.
2. **Secret Baby**: A concealed pregnancy from a past one-night stand reappears 5 years later, upending a powerful dynasty.
3. **Hidden Identity**: A billionaire CEO or royal heir works incognito as an ordinary worker/servant to test loyalty.
4. **Enemies to Lovers**: Ideological opponents forced into mutual survival discover profound mutual respect and desire.
5. **Second Chance**: Former lovers separated by a bitter misunderstanding ten years ago collide again with unhealed wounds.
6. **Revenge Comeback**: A cast-aside victim returns under a new identity with immense resources to bring down their tormentors.
7. **Forbidden Love**: Love between guardian and ward, rival clan heirs, or prisoner and interrogator breaking sacred laws.
8. **Love Triangle**: The protagonist torn between safe, devoted loyalty and dangerous, electric temptation.
9. **Forced Proximity**: Confined together in a blizzard cabin, locked archive, or safehouse stripping away all psychological armor.
10. **Heir Reveal**: The mocked, impoverished outcast is revealed to be the sole legitimate heir to an empire.
11. **Cinderella Rise**: A humble, diligent outsider conquers an aristocratic high-society world through sheer moral purity.
12. **Fake Relationship**: Pretending to date to thwart overbearing parents or provoke jealousy, until the boundary dissolves.
13. **Fated Mates**: An irresistible, cosmic/biological soul bond that defies clan enmity and personal logic.
14. **Amnesia**: Loss of memory forces the protagonist to fall in love with their enemy anew, unaware of a looming dark truth.
15. **Time Travel**: Hurled back into the past with full memory of their tragic death, racing to alter the timeline.
16. **Rebirth**: Second life granted after brutal murder; calculating every step to systematically outplay past killers.
17. **Found Family**: Broken loners banded together by shared trauma, forming a bond stronger than blood royalty.
18. **Redemption Arc**: A cold, villainous enforcer sacrifices their power and pride to protect the one innocent soul they harmed.
