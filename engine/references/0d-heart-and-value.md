# 0d — HEART & VALUE NORTH STAR (Lõi Giá Trị: Cảm Động · Tích Cực · Mang Lại Giá Trị Cho Khán Giả)

> **Authority**: This file states **what every Sven story is for**. All engagement machinery in the engine — hooks, open loops, twists, cold water, face-slap reversals, cliffhangers, debate seeds, retention maps — exists to carry the viewer to this destination. Precedence (`workflow.md` §2.1): below an explicit user instruction and the Hard Constraints, **above** every craft default in the doctrine, Pillars, genre modules, role files and skills. When a retention tool collides with the heart or the value of the story, **change the tool, never the value**.
> **Scope**: every agent and every pipeline — film & series (`@Screenwriter`, `@Director`, `@CombatDirector`, `@PhotoDirector`, `@StoryboardDirector`, `@RefVideoDirector`), product commercials (`@CommercialDirector`), Roblox films (`@RobloxDirector`) and packaging (`@GrowthArchitect`).
> **Kind of rule** (doctrine §0): the North Star (§1), the Dignity & Value Firewall (§5) and the Heart & Value Scorecard pass mark (§8) are **core gates**; the heart mechanics (§4) and genre calibration (§6) are craft defaults that may be broken with `BREAK: <default> — <reason>`.

---

## §1 — THE NORTH STAR (ONE SENTENCE)

**Every Sven story exists to move the viewer and leave them with something good: an emotion they earned, a positive human value they watched someone choose at a cost, and a takeaway they can carry into their own life.**

- **Engagement is the vehicle; value is the destination.** A gripping story that gives the viewer nothing is a failed Sven story. A well-meaning story nobody finishes is also a failure. That is why every checkpoint shows **two** scorecards — *Độ cuốn* (engagement) and *Giá trị* (heart & value) — and both must pass.
- **Value is shown, never preached.** The story answers with a choice, an action and an image (doctrine §8.3, §8.8, §8.9). No character speaks the moral, the takeaway or the title.
- **Dark material is allowed; despair as the destination is not.** Thrillers, horror, tragedy and injustice may be depicted with full weight; the story's meaning still points toward courage, truth, love, responsibility or repair (§7).

---

## §2 — THE THREE PROMISES TO THE VIEWER (BA LỜI HỨA)

| # | Promise | What it means on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cảm động — the viewer is moved** | At least one earned **heart moment** per episode (§4); the story's climax is an emotional peak, not only a plot peak. |
| 2 | **Tích cực — a positive value is affirmed** | A human value is embodied by a character's **costly choice**. Menu (choose by story, never by habit): family love & filial gratitude · kindness to strangers · honesty · responsibility · courage · forgiveness & second chances · perseverance · humility · fairness & justice · protecting the weak · gratitude · self-worth & dignity · solidarity & community · keeping a promise. In most stories `A1`'s **Need** (doctrine §8.4: `Wound ➔ Lie ➔ Want ➔ Need`) *is* the value: the arc is the Lie giving way to it. |
| 3 | **Giá trị mang về — a takeaway** | One sentence the viewer would repeat to someone they love — a new lens on their own life ("The person who scolds you most may be the one most afraid to lose you"). Never spoken on screen. |

## §3 — THE VALUE CARD (THẺ GIÁ TRỊ — appended to the Story Spine, doctrine §8.10)

```
VALUE    | Positive value affirmed: [...] — embodied by [Name]'s choice in [scene / episode]: [...] — at the cost of [...]
HEART    | Heart moment: Cảnh [NN] — [specific act / object / recognition] — cài ở Cảnh [AA] (plant before payoff)
TAKEAWAY | What the viewer carries home (never spoken on screen): "[...]"
SHADOW   | What this story depicts but must not glorify, and how it is handled: [...] — or "Không có"
```

| Stage | What is written |
|---|---|
| Stage 0 concept (`CP0`) / Roblox `GATE R1` concept | `VALUE` + `TAKEAWAY`, one line each, inside the concept card |
| Stage 1 series (`CP1`) / short film Spine / `setting.html` `#story-spine` | all four rows for the whole story |
| Stage 3 episode Pass A (`CP3a`) / Roblox `GATE R2` | `VALUE`, `HEART`, `TAKEAWAY` for this episode (a step toward the series takeaway); `SHADOW` whenever the episode shows cruelty, revenge, deception, violence or humiliation |
| Stage 4 scene | scenes listed in `HEART` are marked `💗` in the scene list and directed per §10 |

## §4 — HEART MECHANICS (HOW A SCENE GENUINELY MOVES — CRAFT, NOT SENTIMENTALITY)

1. **H1 — Specific over grand.** Tears come from one concrete detail only this story has (a hand-warmer left in a coat pocket, a button re-sewn in the wrong colour, a voicemail never deleted) — the `★` item of the Specific Truth Inventory (doctrine §8.5) — never from a big speech or music alone.
2. **H2 — Love that costs and hides.** Goodness moves most when it costs the giver and they hide it. Revealing a hidden kindness late is a twist and a heart moment at once (`TW-10 Hidden Kindness`, `TW-13 Sacrifice`, `TW-04 Perspective`).
3. **H3 — Recognition.** The peak is the instant one character finally **sees** the love, worth or truth in another. Film the face receiving it (doctrine §9.4 empathy mode), not the face giving it.
4. **H4 — Restraint.** Underplay: the character fights the tear, the line gets shorter, silence and one action carry the peak (Model 0, Law D5 Mode 1 wordless final shot). Never stack three crying scenes, never lecture, never let music do the work the scene did not earn.
5. **H5 — Planted, then paid.** A heart moment obeys the twist law: its detail is planted at least one scene earlier (`HEART` row: `cài ở Cảnh AA`). Unplanted sentiment is melodrama.
6. **H6 — Light after pressure.** Warmth lands hardest after cold: the thermal shift cold ➔ warm in the Release, a laugh after grief, an open door after a slammed one.

## §5 — THE DIGNITY & VALUE FIREWALL (TƯỜNG LỬA PHẨM GIÁ — EVERY GENRE)

1. **Justice restores; it never degrades.** A reversal vindicates the wronged person's worth and exposes the wrongdoer's own choices. The payoff is **truth and dignity restored**, not the opponent's humiliation. No kneeling, begging or grovelling as the reward shot; the longest hold goes to the restored person or to the wrongdoer's **realization**, never to their shame.
2. **Consequences are fair and self-caused.** Wrongdoing meets a proportionate consequence (lost trust, legal or financial forfeiture, public truth) produced by the wrongdoer's own earlier choices. The hero does not gloat and is never cruel.
3. **A door left open.** Antagonists keep their humanity (`Where A2 Is Right`, doctrine §1.2 Law 4) and the ending leaves them at least one door toward change — a returned object, an unanswered hand, a chance to repair. Never a forced five-second conversion.
4. **The hero wins by virtue and competence.** Honesty, skill, patience, loyalty, courage, care. Deception that goes unpaid, cruelty and revenge for its own sake never win. An `A1` who starts as an Avenger (`2b` A1-08) travels from revenge toward justice or mercy, and the story shows what revenge costs.
5. **Nothing harmful is made aspirational.** Never glamorize violence, controlling or possessive "love", contempt for the poor, the old, the disabled, women, children or any group, wealth as the measure of a person, cheating as cleverness, or self-harm. Comedy punches at flaws and the powerful, never at the weak. Dark material is depicted with weight and consequence, never as a reward.
6. **Honest packaging.** Titles, thumbnails, hooks and ads promise the heart the story actually delivers; no rage-bait or humiliation imagery as the main promise, no fear- or shame-based selling (Stage 5, commercials).
7. **Minors** stay under the Minor Protection Protocol (`workflow.md` §2.7); stories for young audiences (Roblox) model kindness, honesty and repair.

## §6 — GENRE CALIBRATION (THE HEART EACH GENRE DELIVERS)

Each genre keeps its engagement payoff (doctrine §0.05 Layer 2) and adds a heart & value payoff. Genre Cards carry this as the `Heart & value payoff` row.

| Genre | Engagement payoff (kept) | Heart & value payoff (added) |
|---|---|---|
| Social & family drama | catharsis, earned empathy | reconciliation that costs pride; love, forgiveness, responsibility between generations |
| Healing / slice-of-life | quiet resonance | gratitude, presence, a small kindness that changes a day |
| Romance | an earned union | respect, honesty, two people choosing each other freely (never possessive or controlling love) |
| Microdrama | the status reversal | **dignity reversal**: the overlooked person's worth is recognized, integrity outlasts arrogance, and the crown ends on one act of grace |
| Action & martial arts | adrenaline, tactical victory | courage used to protect; restraint and mercy toward a beaten opponent; force only as the last resort |
| Thriller & crime | intellectual shock | truth and justice; protecting the vulnerable; the cost of a lie |
| Horror | dread, release | courage and solidarity; the haunting ends because someone tells the truth or makes amends |
| Fantasy, rebirth, time-travel | power growth, foresight | power used for others; a second chance spent repairing one's own mistakes and protecting loved ones, not only punishing |
| Sci-fi | wonder, the dilemma | human dignity over the system; responsibility for what we create |
| Sitcom | laughter | warmth: the tag shows a small kindness; the group stays a family after the reset |
| Product commercial | sensory satisfaction | an honest benefit to a real person, family or community; a warm, confident CTA |
| Roblox storytime | the twist, the karma turn | kindness and honesty pay off; wrongdoers face fair consequences and can change; kid-safe and hopeful |

## §7 — ENDINGS AND HOPE

- `ending_mode` stays positive by default; bittersweet or tragic endings remain allowed by user choice (`workflow.md` §2.9).
- **Every ending leaves a light**: a lesson carried by someone, a relationship mended, a promise kept, a wrong owned, a next generation changed. The final image is never meaningless despair.
- Mid-series episodes may end dark (cliffhanger, cold water); the episode still plants a **seed of hope** — a kept promise, a loyal ally, a truth half-seen — so the viewer returns for more than suspense.

## §8 — HEART & VALUE SCORECARD (BẢNG CHẤM GIÁ TRỊ — `/8`)

Score at `CP0` (each concept), `CP1` (series / short film), `CP3a` and `CP3b` (each episode), Roblox `GATE R1` and `GATE R2`, next to the Engagement Scorecard. Each criterion `0`, `1` or `2`. **Pass: `≥ 6 / 8` with no criterion at `0`.** Below the mark, rewrite before presenting the checkpoint.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Giá trị tích cực qua lựa chọn** | no clear value, or the story rewards a harmful one | a value is present but stated in dialogue or costs nothing | a positive value is shown through a character's costly choice |
| 2 | **Khoảnh khắc chạm** | nothing moving, or only melodrama (crying, music, speeches) | moving but generic or unplanted | `≥ 1` specific, planted, restrained heart moment (H1–H5) |
| 3 | **Phẩm giá & công bằng** | humiliation, cruelty or revenge is the payoff, or a group is demeaned | the outcome is fair but the hero gloats or the opponent is flat | dignity restored, consequence fair and self-caused, the opponent humanized with a door left open |
| 4 | **Điều mang về & hy vọng** | nothing to carry home, or a hopeless last image | a takeaway exists but is preached or vague | a clear, unspoken takeaway; the ending (or, mid-series, the cliffhanger) keeps a light of hope |

Canonical line (parsed by `engine/screenplay_audit.py`):
```
💗 BẢNG CHẤM GIÁ TRỊ — Giá trị [ ]/2 · Khoảnh khắc chạm [ ]/2 · Phẩm giá [ ]/2 · Mang về [ ]/2 = [ ]/8
```

## §9 — PRE-FLIGHT: FIVE QUESTIONS BEFORE EVERY CHECKPOINT

1. After the last frame, what will the viewer **feel** — and what will they say to someone they love?
2. Whose choice embodies the value, and what did it **cost** them?
3. Where is the heart moment, and where was it **planted**?
4. Would I be comfortable if a child or a parent **imitated** what the hero does to win?
5. Does the ending — even a dark one — leave a **light**?

## §10 — AGENT DUTIES (ONE LINE EACH)

| Agent | Duty |
|---|---|
| Sven-Agent (Showrunner) | Shows both scorecards at every gate; never presents `CP0`, `CP1`, `CP3a` or `CP3b` below `6/8` or with a criterion at `0`. |
| `@Screenwriter` | Writes the Value Card into every Spine, marks heart scenes `💗` in the scene list, builds the crisis choice so the value costs something. |
| `@Director`, `@PhotoDirector`, `@StoryboardDirector` | On a `💗` scene: give the beat room — static camera or one slow motivated push-in, hold the receiving face, keep the final shot wordless (Law D5 Mode 1), let the light warm; never cut away from the recognition. |
| `@CombatDirector` | Force protects someone; every fight carries a moral turn (restraint, mercy, a rescue); never linger on suffering. |
| `@CommercialDirector` | Sells an honest benefit through a person it helps; warm CTA; no fear, shame or body-shaming hooks. |
| `@RefVideoDirector` | Swaps never turn a source clip into mockery of a real person or group. |
| `@RobloxDirector` | Storytime morals land through events, not sermons; kindness and honesty pay off; fair, non-humiliating consequences; kid-safe. |
| `@GrowthArchitect` | Titles and thumbnails sell the heart honestly; injustice may hook, but the promise is the repair, not the humiliation. |
