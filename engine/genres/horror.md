## 1. The Dread Equation

$$\text{Horror} = \text{A Rule of the Haunting} + \text{Withheld Information} + \text{A Human Secret the Haunting Feeds On}$$

1. **The haunting has rules** (`Trigger – Ceiling – Cost`, shared with `fantasy.md`): what wakes it, what it cannot do, what it takes. The audience learns them through victims' near-misses, not exposition.
2. **The camera withholds, never lies** (Doctrine §9.6): frame away, hold on the listener, let sound arrive before image. Never show the full entity before the climax.
3. **The real horror is human** — a buried guilt, a broken promise, a family secret. The supernatural is the pressure that forces it out; the ending answers the human question.

## 2. Dread Mechanics (screenplay side)

- **Build ➔ false release ➔ real shock**: every shock is preceded by at least one scene of build and one false alarm; never two shocks in consecutive scenes.
- **Sound first**: write the off-screen sound (`[sound]:` cue in Stage 4) before the image that explains it; silence is the loudest beat before the shock.
- **Rule of the empty frame**: a doorway, a mirror, the dark end of a corridor — hold it in a `15.0s` clip so the viewer searches the frame.
- **Folk specificity**: rituals, offerings, taboos and sayings come from the real culture of the setting (Doctrine §8.5); never invent rituals that misrepresent it.
- **Safety proxies**: show consequence through objects and reactions — a dropped incense stick, a child's sandal at the water's edge (no child in danger on screen), a phone screen freezing on a face — never bodies or blood.

## 3. Episode Shape (default sketch — flex per 0c §4)

| Phase | Function |
|---|---|
| Open (15–20%) | Normal ritual of the place, then one wrong detail (hook) |
| Develop (40–50%) | Rules surface through near-misses; the human secret starts to leak; one false alarm |
| Climax (20–25%) | The rule is broken; the shock lands; the secret is exposed in the same beat |
| Close (10%) | Quiet aftermath, then a sign the haunting is not finished (cliffhanger) |

## 4. Pre-Flight Additions
1. Is every shock preceded by build and a false release?
2. Can the haunting's rules be stated, and were they shown before they mattered?
3. What human secret does the haunting expose, and does the ending answer it?
4. Is every frightening beat free of gore, graphic death and minors in danger?
