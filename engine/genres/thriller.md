## 0. Core Principles & Dramatic Philosophy

The **Psychological Thriller, Detective Mystery, Folk Suspense & Survival Mind-Games Genre Engine** governs high-tension cerebral and atmospheric storytelling where **information asymmetry, visual clues, ticking clocks, and sudden physical pursuits** keep the viewer on the edge of their seat.

### 0.1 The Thriller-Mystery Equation
$$\text{Masterwork Thriller} = \text{Knowledge Matrix Manipulation (Suspense} \leftrightarrow \text{Mystery)} + \text{Fair-Play Visual Clues (Planted Early)} + \text{Chiaroscuro Staging} + \text{Seedance (10s/15s) Pursuit/Escape Peaks}$$

1. **The Knowledge Matrix Switch (`Suspense` vs. `Mystery` vs. `The Prestige Reveal` — Doctrine §9)**:
   - **Suspense Mode (`Audience > Character`)**: The viewer sees the planted danger (the shadow behind the frosted glass, the tampered lock, the accomplice pocketing the key) before `A1` does, generating suffocating anticipation.
   - **Mystery Mode (`Character ≥ Audience`)**: `A1` notices a contradiction in the crime scene or alibi and executes a covert test without explaining it aloud until the trap snaps shut.
   - **The Prestige Switch**: Every episode alternates intentionally between Suspense and Mystery, culminating in an **Option C Reveal** that breaks the viewer's initial prediction while honoring every planted visual clue.
2. **The Fair-Play Visual Clue Law (`Show, Never Point-and-Explain`)**:
   - Every major reversal (`TW-01..20`) must be preceded by **$\ge 2$ non-verbal visual plants** in earlier scenes (an off-hand prop placement, a reflection in a mirror, mud on a shoe heel, a trembling finger).
   - Characters are **strictly forbidden** from pointing at clues or delivering awkward expository monologues during the plant phase; clues are woven naturally into foreground/background blocking.
3. **100% Filter-Safe Psychological Dread & Forensic Tension**:
   - Upholds Sven's **Zero-Fatality, Zero-Gore & Minor Protection Mandate**: Crime, folk mystery, and survival stakes operate through **abduction, framed guilt, stolen evidence, locked-room traps, karmic illusions, and imminent peril**—strictly zero corpses, zero blood pools, and zero graphic violence.

## 2. The 5 Sovereign Rules of the Thriller & Mystery Engine

### Rule 1: Ratio of Silence to Scalpel Dialogue (`70% Visual/Acoustic Tension — 30% Spoken Scalpels`)
- Thriller characters never ramble. Dialogue lines act as **loaded scalpels** ($\le 12\text{–}14$ words): testing an alibi, baiting a slip of the tongue, or masking panic.
- Frequently deploy **`Model 0 (Pure Somatic & Diegetic Silence)`** (`CADENCE: PURE_VISUAL_STEALTH` or `CLAUSTROPHOBIC_STANDOFF`) and **`O.S. (Off-Screen Auditory Intrusion)`** (a floorboard creak, a metallic latch clicking shut, footsteps stopping outside the door).

### Rule 2: Chiaroscuro Lighting & Foreground Occlusion Staging (`@Director` Pillar 3)
- Direct every thriller scene with high-contrast **Chiaroscuro lighting** (venetian blind slash shadows, single desk lamp pools in darkness, cold rain-streaked window rim light, or flickering amber lantern glow against slate-blue mist).
- Use **Foreground Architectural Occlusion** (shooting past a half-open door frame, bookshelf gap, or frosted glass partition) to create voyeuristic tension and spatial depth.

### Rule 3: Kinetic Release Peaks When the Story Earns Them (`Seedance 10.0s / 15.0s`)
When the plot naturally produces a pursuit, escape or struggle, break the quiet cerebral pressure with **`1–2` kinetic release scenes (`10.0s` or `15.0s`)**. A cerebral episode (interrogation, locked room, psychological duel) may have none — never insert a chase only to fill a quota. Flag them in Stage 3 as:
- **`[⚡ MODEL KHUYẾN NGHỊ: SEEDANCE · 10.0s / 15.0s]`**
- Examples: Rain-soaked alley pursuit after an unmasked suspect, desperate stealth sprint before a vault door seals, close-quarters physical struggle over a Tier-1 forensic ledger/evidence drive, or fleeing through a mist-choked forest in a folk thriller.

### Rule 4: The 3-Layer Alibi & `Option C` Culprit Reversal
- **Suspect 1 (The Obvious Red Herring)**: Cleared or exposed as a pawn by the Midpoint (`Scene 05`).
- **Suspect 2 (The Compromised Satellite / `B-Judas`)**: Holds a real secret (`C-Plot`) that collides with the investigation in `Scene 07–08`.
- **The Prestige Reveal (`Option C`)**: The true mechanism or mastermind move connects the two previously ignored visual plants from `Scenes 02 & 04`.

### Rule 5: Seamless `TH1 / TH2 / TH3` Continuity Discipline
- Use **`TH1` (Static Last-Frame Reference)** across continuous interrogation rooms, stealth infiltration corridors, and multi-scene pursuits so lighting, prop positions, and character sweat/posture never reset.
- Use **`TH2` (New Asset Anchor)** when cutting between parallel POVs (e.g., Detective `A1` in the archive vs. Mastermind `A2` in the control room — `Bridge 3 Parallel Counter-Strike`).
- Use **`TH3` (Split Subject-Context Shift)** in Folk Suspense or Psychological Thrillers when a character's hallucination, memory reconstruction, or illusion array shatters to reveal the real room around them.
