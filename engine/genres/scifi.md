## 1. The Sci-Fi Equation

$$\text{Sci-Fi Drama} = \text{One "What If?"} + \text{3 Hard World Rules} + \text{A Human Price Only This World Could Charge}$$

1. **One premise, fully explored** — one invention or shift (memory can be sold, an AI can testify, the city rations daylight). Never stack three unrelated novelties.
2. **The 3 Hard World Rules** — locked at Stage 1 and shown on screen before they matter:
   - *Mechanism*: what the technology does, in one observable sentence.
   - *Failure limit*: when and how it breaks (battery, range, latency, side-effect).
   - *Human cost*: what using it takes from a person (memory, trust, privacy, a body part, a relationship).
3. **The dilemma comes from the rules** — the crisis choice of each episode must be impossible in our world and inevitable in this one.
4. **Tech never wins the climax by itself** — `A1` wins by a choice the rules make costly (Doctrine §9.1).

## 2. Story Rules

- **Plant every rule before it pays off** (`≥ 2` plants for the rule that decides the finale) — the rewatch test applies to world rules too (Doctrine §9.7).
- **Show the rule in a tactile beat**, never in a lecture: a gauge dropping, a memory chip warm in the palm, a door that refuses a fingerprint. One insert beats one paragraph of explanation.
- **`A2` holds the strongest version of the other side** of the technology question (efficiency vs. dignity, safety vs. freedom).
- **Keep the human scale** — one family, one crew, one block; the world shows through their objects and routines (Specific Truth Inventory, Doctrine §8.5).
- **Tier-1 prop**: the device or sample is usually the hero prop, with a 3-state evolution (`intact ➔ activated/failing ➔ broken/reforged`).

## 3. Episode Shape (default sketch — flex per 0c §4)

| Phase | Function |
|---|---|
| Open (15–20%) | The rule seen working in an ordinary moment, then a glitch (hook) |
| Develop (40–50%) | `A1` exploits or hides the rule; the cost starts to show; `A2` counters with the same rule |
| Climax (20–25%) | The failure limit hits at the worst moment; crisis choice priced by the human cost |
| Close (10%) | The new rule-state of the world + a cliffhanger that reframes who controls the technology |

High-velocity containment breaches, pursuits or zero-gravity struggles go to `@CombatDirector` (`[⚡ SEEDANCE]`) **only when the plot produces them**.

## 4. Pre-Flight Additions
1. Can each of the 3 world rules be stated in one sentence and pointed to on screen?
2. Was the deciding rule planted at least twice before it decided anything?
3. Is the episode's crisis choice impossible without the premise?
4. Does any line explain the technology that an insert could show instead?
