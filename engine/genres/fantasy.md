## 0. Core Principles & Dramatic Philosophy

The **Time-Travel, Portal Isekai, Cultivation & Supernatural Fantasy Genre Engine** is engineered to power high-retention, visually breathtaking speculative cinema — particularly tailored for **`3D Semi-Realistic Animation (Hoạt Hình 3D Bán Chân Thực — Mẫu 2B)`**, **`Chinese Xianxia 3D Donghua`**, and high-concept live-action fantasy.

In the Sven Cinematic Universe, **supernatural mechanics, time-loops, and cultivation realms are never hollow CGI spectacle**. Every portal jump, rebirth, or awakened bloodline exists to test a deeply human **Core Wound** and **Moral Dilemma** (*What would you sacrifice if given a second life, or what corrupts the human heart when granted godlike power?*).

### 0.1 The Speculative Fantasy Equation
$$\text{Addictive Fantasy Drama} = \text{Asymmetric Knowledge / Awakened Power Advantage} + \text{TH3 Spatial-Temporal Shift Mechanics} + \text{Escalating Realm / Timeline Stakes} + \text{Seedance (10s/15s) Kinetic Climax}$$

1. **The Asymmetric Advantage & Ticking Cost Law (Lợi Thế Bất Đối Xứng & Cái Giá Phải Trả)**:
   - Whether through **Rebirth (`Trùng Sinh`)** (retaining future memories of betrayal), **Time-Travel / Isekai (`Xuyên Không`)** (bringing modern forensic/medical/tactical intellect into an ancient or parallel world), or **System / Bloodline Awakening (`Thức Tỉnh Dị Năng`)**, the protagonist (`A1`) possesses an asymmetric cognitive or supernatural edge.
   - However, every use of that edge carries a **Ticking Cost** (butterfly-effect timeline divergence, spiritual qi backlash, karmic debt, or an overarching apex hunter `A2` who notices the anomaly).
2. **Native Integration of the `TH3` Continuity Reference Protocol (`Điểm Nối Tham Chiếu Xuyên Không / Biến Đổi Không Gian`)**:
   - Fantasy and Time-Travel stories frequently execute **instant spatial or dimensional transitions** mid-action (stepping through a portal, triggering an ancestral jade talisman, shattering an illusion array, or leaping from a modern rooftop into an ancient battlefield).
   - At every spatial shift boundary (`Scene K-1 ➔ Scene K`), enforce **Case 3 (`TH3` — Split Subject-Context Continuity)**:
     * **`[subject]:` & `[prop]:` (Continuous Somatic & Momentum Lock from Scene $K-1$ Last Frame `@00:10.0 / @00:15.0`)**: Lock `A1`'s exact body posture, forward momentum vector, facial micro-expression,active hand-grip, and **Surface-Adhered VFX (`tightly clinging and physically adhered to garment hems and blade edge`)** from the **Static Terminal Frame of Scene $K-1$** alongside the Character Reference Sheet (`<<<Image 2>>> (fully_preserved - same female/male face, slender feminine / masculine body proportions...)` + Line 2 `<Name>'s skin, hands (and makeup): fully_preserved - ...`).
     * **`[context]:` (Instant New World / Era Anchor)**: Discard the old room's environment completely and anchor `[context]:` 100% to the **New Era / Realm / Dungeon Location Reference Sheet (`<<<Image 1>>> (fully_preserved - same architectural layout...)`)**, adding observable environmental transition physics in `Shot 1` (e.g., dissipating golden temporal ripples, swirling mist, or portal shockwaves).
3. **First-Class Synergy with `3D Semi-Realistic Animation (Mẫu 2B)`**:
   - Unless overridden by the user, default visual styling to **`3d-semi-real`** (*Light Chaser Animation / Blur Studio style*): true `7.5–8 head` proportions, sculpted 3D facial bone structure, refined subsurface scattering (SSS) satin skin, expressive luminous eyes, strand-based 3D hair grooming, and PBR silk/armor/relic shaders.

## 2. The 5 Sovereign Rules of the Fantasy & Time-Travel Engine

### Rule 1: The Concrete World-Law Contract (Luật Giới Hạn Hệ Thống Sức Mạnh & Thời Không)
Magic, cultivation, or time-travel must never feel like arbitrary `deus ex machina`:
- In **Stage 1 (`Kịch bản chính`)** and **Stage 2 (`setting.html` `#story`)**, lock **3 Hard World Rules**:
  1. **The Trigger**: What concrete physical action or Tier-1 Relic activates the time-jump, portal, or supernatural surge?
  2. **The Ceiling**: What can this power *not* do in the current Act?
  3. **The Cost / Cooldown**: What visible somatic or material toll occurs after use (e.g., frost creeping up the fingertips, hairline fractures appearing on the jade talisman, breath steaming cold)?

### Rule 2: Tier-1 Soul Relic & Weapon Mutability (`3-State Artifact Evolution`)
Every fantasy/time-travel series anchors its visual identity around **1–2 Tier-1 Hero Relics or Weapons** in `setting.html` Section 4A:
- **State 0 (Dormant / Mundane Seal)**: Ancient weathered bronze sword, dull cracked jade pendant, or dormant silver pocket watch.
- **State 1 (Awakened / Resonance Active)**: Emits observable physical light/particle physics (`faint cyan spiritual runes glowing along the blade fuller`, `golden temporal dust orbiting the open watch dial`).
- **State 2 (Overdrive / Shattered Transformation)**: Renamed entity state when pushed past the limit (`Shattered Jade Core Fragments`, `Blazing Crimson Lotus Greatsword`).

### Rule 3: Kinetic Action & Chase Peaks When the Story Earns Them (`Seedance 10.0s / 15.0s`)
Action-led sub-genres (cultivation, portal survival) usually carry **`1–2` kinetic action, chase, ambush or supernatural clash scenes (`10.0s` or `15.0s`)** per episode. Court-intrigue `Trùng Sinh` or romance-heavy `Xuyên Không` episodes use them only when the plot produces the clash — never as a quota. Tag them in Stage 3 as:
- **`[⚡ MODEL KHUYẾN NGHỊ: SEEDANCE · 10.0s / 15.0s]`**
- Examples: Rooftop tile pursuit across a lantern-lit palace, escaping a collapsing dimensional rift, carriage/steed ambush in a bamboo forest, or a `2.3x–10x` martial/supernatural duel directed with `@CombatDirector`.

### Rule 4: Multi-Wardrobe / Dual-Era Character Sheet Protocol (`setting.html` Section 3)
When a character travels between two distinct eras (e.g., Modern City $\leftrightarrow$ Ancient Dynasty) or undergoes an Awakening transformation:
- Create **separate, explicitly numbered 16:9 Model Sheets (`Mẫu 2B`)** for each major era/form (e.g., `Image 1: ho_ten (Modern Attire)` vs. `Image 2: ho_ten (Ancient Silk Hanfu / Combat Armor)`).
- Never ask the AI video model to invent a costume change via text alone if the character remains in that new era across multiple scenes; only use **`TH3` mid-scene transformation** for the exact 10s clip where the spatial jump or awakening physically occurs.

### Rule 5: 100% Filter-Safe Supernatural Spectacle
- Uphold Sven's **Zero-Fatality, Zero-Gore & Minor Protection Law**: Supernatural strikes, curses, and blade clashes manifest through **elemental light shockwaves, crystalline frost, golden qi ripples, shattered armor plates, and environmental cratering**—never blood spray, dismemberment, or lethal gore.

## Mastery Playbook — Fantasy, Time-Travel & Rebirth (README §2c)

### P1. The promise and the three killers
- **Promise**: a person thrown into a bigger world (another era, a cultivation realm, a second life) who uses what only they know or can do — and pays a price the old world never charged.
- **Killer 1 — power without cost**: every ability has a trigger, a ceiling and a price (World-Law Contract); an ability that solves everything ends the story.
- **Killer 2 — the world explained instead of shown**: rules appear through a near-miss, a ritual or an object, never through a lecture or a system window full of text.
- **Killer 3 — the future-knowledge cheat**: in rebirth/time-travel, knowledge of the future must start failing by the midpoint (butterfly effect), or there is no suspense.

### P2. Series architecture
| Act | Job |
|---|---|
| I Crossing | The crossing or awakening in the first episode; the hero's edge (future knowledge, a hidden talent, a relic) and its first price |
| IIA Rising | The edge wins small battles; enemies notice; allies form; the relic evolves `State 0 ➔ 1` |
| IIB Midpoint | The edge breaks: the future changes, the talent backfires, the relic cracks (`State 2`); a secret about the hero's origin |
| IIIA Fall | The cost comes due (a loved one, memory, lifespan, status); the hero must fight without the edge |
| IIIB+IV Ascension | Victory through a choice the old self would not make; the world-law is honoured, not cheated; closing image across eras/realms |
- **Power ledger**: each ability — trigger, ceiling, price, scene where each is first shown.
- **Divergence ledger** (rebirth/time-travel): what the hero knows will happen, and the episode where it stops happening.

### P3. Episode architecture (default ~10 scenes)
| Scenes | Function |
|---|---|
| 01 | A visual paradox of the world (a modern habit in an ancient court, a relic pulsing) |
| 02–03 | The hero uses the edge on a small problem; the price shows |
| 04 | A rule of the world revealed in a near-miss |
| 05–06 | An opponent counters with the same world's rules |
| 07 | Clash: kinetic only if the plot produces it; otherwise a court/strategic duel |
| 08 | Reversal in front of witnesses |
| 09 | The cost lands on the body or a relationship |
| 10 | A higher threat or a divergence from the known future |

### P4. Signature scene: the knowledge duel
- The hero says something only someone from "outside" could know; the court/sect/family freezes. The camera holds on whoever understands what that implies.
- Pair it with a tactile tell of the price (a nosebleed, a fading tattoo, a cracked relic) so the win is never free.

### P5. Dialogue
- Period or sect speech register stays consistent; the hero's modern slips are rare and deliberate.
- Example:
  > *Lăng Vân rót trà, tay trái vô thức xoay chiếc nhẫn ngọc vừa nứt thêm một vệt.*
  > **Hoàng hậu:** "Muội biết trước cả việc đê vỡ ở Giang Nam?"
  > **Lăng Vân:** "Thần thiếp chỉ biết nước sông dâng."
  > *Hoàng hậu đặt chén xuống. Vệt nứt trên nhẫn lan thêm một chút.*

### P6. Hooks, twists, cliffhangers
- Hooks: #1 Visual Paradox, #8 Overlapping Time Traces, #3 Strange Object/Sign.
- Twists: TW-18 Time Shift, TW-16 Secret Connection, TW-19 Symbolic, TW-05 Identity.
- Cliffhangers: C7 Ghost's Arrival (someone from the "other" life), C1 Identity Unmasking, divergence from the known future.

### P7. Mastery checklist
1. Did every power used this episode show its price on screen?
2. Was the world rule that decided the scene shown before it decided it?
3. Has the hero's edge started to fail by the midpoint of the series?
4. Is the relic's state the hero's inner state?
5. Is no rule explained in dialogue that an object or ritual could show?
