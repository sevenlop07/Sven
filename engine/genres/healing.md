## Mastery Playbook — Healing & Slice-of-Life (README §2c)

### P1. The promise and the three killers
- **Promise**: an ordinary life seen so precisely that the viewer recognises themselves, and one small inner knot that loosens.
- **Killer 1 — nothing changes**: calm is not the same as static; every episode still shifts one value inside a character.
- **Killer 2 — telling the feeling**: no line names the healing; a hand, a dish, a habit shows it.
- **Killer 3 — borrowed drama**: importing villains, accidents or shouting matches breaks the contract with this audience.

### P2. Series architecture (four movements)
| Movement | Job |
|---|---|
| Ki — Solitude | `A1`'s routine and the small wound inside it (a habit kept for someone gone, a skill abandoned) |
| Shō — Connection | Someone or something enters the routine (a neighbour, a stray cat, a returning relative); small kindnesses |
| Ten — The test | Life shifts: a storm, an illness, a move, an old letter — the wound is touched |
| Ketsu — Resonance | `A1` does the thing they could not do in Ki; a wordless closing image answers the opening one |
- In a series, each episode is a mini Ki-Shō-Ten-Ketsu, and the season as a whole is one larger one.
- **Specific truth inventory** is everything here: 10 lived details (sounds, food, objects, rituals) — at least 5 on screen per episode.

### P3. Episode architecture
| Scenes | Function |
|---|---|
| 01 | Atmosphere hook: a precise sensory detail of the routine |
| 02–03 | The routine and the small wound inside it |
| 04–05 | A connection: someone or something new, a small exchange |
| 06 | Silent scene: hands at work, ambient sound |
| 07–08 | The test: a small turn that touches the wound |
| 09 | The gesture: `A1` changes one habit |
| 10 | Wordless resonance image + a gentle open question |

### P4. Signature scene: hands at work
- A `15s` hold on a task done with care (cooking, repairing, sewing, watering plants) while the emotion shifts on the face. The task carries the meaning.
- Ambient sound is a character: rain on the tin roof, a kettle, a radio from next door.

### P5. Dialogue
- Few words; what matters is said sideways, through food, weather or memories.
- Example:
  > *Ông cụ múc thêm một vá canh, đẩy bát về phía cậu sinh viên thuê trọ.*
  > **Ông cụ:** "Nấu nhiều quá, đổ đi thì phí."
  > **Minh:** "Hôm qua ông cũng nói vậy."
  > *Ông cụ cười, không đáp. Trên bàn vẫn còn hai cái bát.*

### P6. Hooks, twists, endings
- Hooks: #5 Atmospheric Contrast, #6 Body Detail, #11 Diegetic Sound.
- Turns: TW-10 Hidden Kindness, TW-03 Emotional, TW-11 Full Circle.
- Episode endings: a gentle open question, never a shock.

### P7. Mastery checklist
1. Does one inner value shift, even if nothing loud happens?
2. Are at least 5 specific lived details on screen?
3. Is there a silent "hands at work" scene?
4. Does no line name the feeling or the lesson?
5. Does the closing image answer the opening one?
