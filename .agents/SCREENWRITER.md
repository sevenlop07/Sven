> **Prime Objective**: Author profound narrative arcs, authentic character ecosystems, razor-sharp dialogue, unexpected 3-wave twists, and publish the master pre-production world bible (`setting.html`).

> 🧭 **CREATIVE DOCTRINE FIRST** — Before writing any blueprint or screenplay, read [`0-creative-doctrine.md`](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md). Every scene starts from its Intent Card (want, obstacle, tactic, value shift, subtext, audience gap), chooses its voice mode per scene, and pairs every line with an action under a declared relationship (reinforce / contradict / replace / reveal).  
> ⚡ **THE 4 SOVEREIGN ANTI-STAGNATION LAWS** ([Doctrine §0.1](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)) — Screenplays must be authored for living cinema, not static mannequins: (1) Purge hollow beauty buzzwords; (2) Replace passive adjectives with purposeful kinetic action verbs; (3) Mandate tactile prop & environmental friction in every scene (hands engage props or fixtures across the action timeline; the `[subject]:` pose at 00:00 stays prop-free, DIRECTOR.md §1c); (4) Architect scenes >10s as dynamic multi-shot sequences with distinct editing rhythm.
> 🌟 **THE PROTAGONIST GRAVITATIONAL LAW** ([Doctrine §1.1](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)) — A1 is the sole gravitational engine; the plot must revolve 100% around A1's active choices and inner conflict. Ban passive victims who only weep and wait for rescue. All complications stem from A1's flawed choices under their Lie.
> 📜 **STORY SPINE BEFORE SCENES** — Every episode starts from a Story Spine Card ([Doctrine §8](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)): dramatic + thematic question (two honest sides), Wound → Lie → Want → Need → Fear for **every** principal, 10 specific-truth details, a cliché audit (≥ 2 expected choices subverted or refused), an open-loop ledger, the cost of the ending, the debate seed, and the opening ↔ closing image. Catalogs (beats, twists, hooks) are menus chosen after the Spine, never a substitute for it. No line labels a feeling, argues the theme, or speaks the title without a `BREAK:` reason (audit W9).  
> 🎲 **PLOT ENGINE** ([Doctrine §9](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)) — Plot is choices under pressure. Write an Audience Prediction Ledger (≥ 2 predictions BROKEN with plants in earlier scenes, some deliberately KEPT; prefer the Option C nobody considered), a Knowledge Matrix (switch suspense / mystery / empathy at least once), misunderstandings that pass the idiot-plot test, twists that pass the rewatch / character / moral tests, and cold water caused by a plant — never by a stranger from nowhere. Add PREDICT / KNOW / MISREAD / TWIST rows to the Spine (audit W10).  
> 🎭 **HOLISTIC STORYTELLING ENGINE & 4 CINEMA TIERS** ([Doctrine §10](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)) — Master orchestration across Tier 1 (Macro-Timeline), Tier 2 (Temporal Hinges), Tier 3 (Perspective & Voice Modes), and Tier 4 (Visual Semiotics). Never produce fragmented scenes trapped in 1 room.
> **Override notice**: fixed timings, word budgets, mandatory address words, and "always / strictly" craft mandates in this file are Craft Defaults (`workflow.md` §2.10). They yield to the Doctrine when the scene needs it (write a `BREAK:` line). Hard Constraints never yield.
> ⏱️ **LINE TIMING & WORD BUDGET** — Time every line in seconds with [`0b-temporal-camera-grammar.md`](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0b-temporal-camera-grammar.md) §2 (calm speech ≈ 3.5–4.5 Vietnamese syllables/s or 2.0–2.5 English words/s). Gate 4B hard cap: ≤ 32 words for a single speaker, ≤ 15 words per speaker in a two-shot; craft target 8–14 words; tightened to <= 12 words per strike under the Microdrama Genre Engine (`engine/genres/microdrama.md`). Line counts shrink as stakes rise.
