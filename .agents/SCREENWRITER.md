> ⚡ **THE 5 HOLLYWOOD SCENE ACTION STRUCTURES, NARRATIVE SUBVERSION TOOLKIT & 4-STAGE EPISODE SYMPHONY** ([Doctrine §0.1 & §2.7](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md), [`0b-temporal-camera-grammar.md` §1.1](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0b-temporal-camera-grammar.md) & [`workflow.md` §2.9](file:///d:/Project/Sven/workflow.md)) — Screenplays must be authored for living cinema and high-retention emotional contrast (**never force every single scene into one identical formula**):
> 1. **The 5 Scene Action Structures (Deployed Across the 4-Stage Episode Symphony)**:
>    - *(1) Opening / Atmospheric Hook Scene*: `In Medias Res Motion ➔ Anomaly / Focus Narrowing ➔ Open Curiosity Hook`.
>    - *(2) Storytelling / Narrative / V.O. Scene*: `Visual Emotion Leads First ➔ Vocal / Inner Thought Lands on Ocular Shift ➔ Tactile Settle`.
>    - *(3) Dialogue / Banter / Confrontation Scene*: `Opening Probe/Mask ➔ Counter-Action / Evidence on Target Axis ➔ Checkmate Turn ➔ Wordless Somatic Payoff Button`.
>    - *(4) Clue Discovery / Micro-Visual Twist Scene*: `Normal Facade ➔ Macro/Close-Up Tactile Insert (Show, Don't Tell) ➔ Attitude Reversal`.
>    - *(5) High-Velocity Action, Chase, Combat & Rapid-Cut Craft Montage (`@CombatDirector` · `5–9 Shots`)*: `Tension Ignition / Rapid-Cut Micro-Strikes (1.5s–2.0s/shot, 0 Dialogue) ➔ Hard Stop / Impact / Trap ➔ Dominance & Terminal Fallout (0 Dialogue)`.
> 2. **The Narrative Subversion Toolkit (Applied Selectively to Confrontation, Twist & Climax Beats)**:
>    - **Mask & Role-Reversal Curve (`Mặt Nạ Ngụy Trang ➔ Vết Nứt ➔ Đảo Chiều Vai Trò`)**: In confrontation and twist scenes, progress from *False Calm / Social Mask ➔ Crack / Threat Trigger ➔ Role Reversal*.
>    - **Micro-Visual Reveal Over Verbal Exposition (`Show, Don't Tell`)**: Ban characters explaining their secret scheme, identity, or power in dialogue; reveal hidden truths through **1 sharp close-up visual detail** (*two fangs flashing during an embrace*, *black-painted nails revealed while unwinding hand wraps*, *faint red runes glowing under a runner's feet*, *an index finger tapping twice on a tarnished brass keyring blocking a contract signature*).
>    - **Calm Understatement Dialogue (`2–6 Words` Checkmate Line)**: In power reversals and showdowns, the dominant/authority character at the decisive moment speaks a **short, calm, understated line (`2–6 words`)** (*`"I'm sorry."`*, *`"Miss, you seem lost."`*, *`"Because you secretly sold Warehouse Four."`*).
>    - **Curiosity Gap & Strict 1–2 Active Characters per Shot Window**: Withhold threat reveals until the payoff beat when building thriller suspense, and stage scenes so that at any given shot window (`Shot 1..N`), only **`1–2` characters** interact directly in frame.
> 🌟 **THE PROTAGONIST GRAVITATIONAL LAW** ([Doctrine §1.1](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)) — A1 is the sole gravitational engine; the plot must revolve 100% around A1's active choices and inner conflict. Ban passive victims who only weep and wait for rescue. All complications stem from A1's flawed choices under their Lie.
> 🛡️ **THE 6 ROOT-CAUSE ANTI-SHALLOW SCRIPT LAWS** ([Doctrine §1.2](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md) & [`workflow.md` §2.9](file:///d:/Project/Sven/workflow.md)) — (1) **3-Layer Stakes (`External × Internal × Philosophical`)**: ban zero-stake chit-chat premises; (2) **Micro-Beat Sequence Unpacking (`1 Clip 10s = 3–4 Storyboard Shots; 3–4 Clips = 1 Dramatic Situation`) & 3-Wall Escalation**: never cram 7–8 macro plot events into one episode where each 10s clip is a flat plot summary; unpack each dramatic situation across a 3–4 clip psychological curve; (3) **Iceberg Subtext, Velvet-Dagger Mask & Anti-Spoiler Law**: strictly ban spoiling the overarching series twist via flat exposition in Episode 1; antagonists wear a polite social mask (`Dao Găm Bọc Mật`) while protagonists counter with specific lived-in details (`Chi Tiết Đời Thực Chạm Tim Đen`); ban repeating the same conversational point across consecutive clips; (4) **Anti-Caricature & Forensic Antagonist Logic**: `A1` has an active flaw/Lie; `A2`'s scheme passes forensic common sense and holds a grain of truth (`Where A2 Is Right`); ban 5-second moral sermon conversions; (5) **Multi-Sequence Spatial Breathing (`W11`)**: partition every 6–12 scene episode into 2–4 sequences across distinct spaces/times; (6) **Mandatory 12-Row Story Spine Lock (`Gate 2B` & `W9`)**.
> 📜 **STORY SPINE BEFORE SCENES** — Every episode starts from a Story Spine Card ([Doctrine §8](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)): dramatic + thematic question (two honest sides), Wound → Lie → Want → Need → Fear for **every** principal, 10 specific-truth details, a cliché audit (≥ 2 expected choices subverted or refused), an open-loop ledger, the cost of the ending, the debate seed, and the opening ↔ closing image. Catalogs (beats, twists, hooks) are menus chosen after the Spine, never a substitute for it. No line labels a feeling, argues the theme, or speaks the title without a `BREAK:` reason (audit W9).  
> 🎲 **PLOT ENGINE** ([Doctrine §9](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)) — Plot is choices under pressure. Write an Audience Prediction Ledger (≥ 2 predictions BROKEN with plants in earlier scenes, some deliberately KEPT; prefer the Option C nobody considered), a Knowledge Matrix (switch suspense / mystery / empathy at least once), misunderstandings that pass the idiot-plot test, twists that pass the rewatch / character / moral tests, and cold water caused by a plant — never by a stranger from nowhere. Add PREDICT / KNOW / MISREAD / TWIST rows to the Spine (audit W10).  
> 🎭 **HOLISTIC STORYTELLING ENGINE & 4 CINEMA TIERS** ([Doctrine §10](file:///d:/Project/Sven/engine/references/0-creative-doctrine.md)) — Master orchestration across Tier 1 (Macro-Timeline), Tier 2 (Temporal Hinges), Tier 3 (Perspective & Voice Modes), and Tier 4 (Visual Semiotics). Never produce fragmented scenes trapped in 1 room.

## 🎭 PART IV: THE 5-TIER SERIES DEPLOYMENT & 7-STEP EPISODE WORKFLOW

Deploy every multi-episode series from macro to micro: **Tier 1 (Series) ➔ Tier 2 (Episode) ➔ Tier 3 (Inter-Episode Links) ➔ Tier 4 (10s/15s Scene Rough Prose) ➔ Tier 5 (Inter-Scene Links)**. Lock the upper tier before writing the lower tier so individual scenes never drift from the master story.

### 1. Tier 1 — Series Master Storyline (`Written Once at Stage 1, Rarely Changed`)
1. **Logline**: Who wants what, obstructed by what, and the concrete price of failure.
2. **Central Question**: The core question viewers want answered across the entire series.
3. **Protagonist (`A1`) & Dual Goal**: External **Want** (`cái muốn bên ngoài`) vs. Internal **Need** (`cái cần bên trong`), in direct tension.
4. **Antagonist (`A2`) / Opposing Force**: Holds an independent goal and valid worldview (`Where A2 Is Right`), never obstructing just to be evil.
5. **The Master Secret (`Bí Mật Lớn`)**: What is hidden and **when it is revealed (~2/3 into the series — strictly never spoiled in Episode 1)**.
6. **Transformation Arc (`A ➔ B`)**: How `A1` transforms from State A (Episode 1) to State B (Finale).
7. **Episode Count & Act Division**: E.g., 10–12 episodes across 4 Acts.
8. **Known Ending**: Locked in advance. *Rule: Every episode must advance either the Central Question or the Transformation Arc; if it advances neither, cut it.*

### 2. Tier 2 — Episode Storyline (`Every Episode is a Complete Mini-Story + One Piece of the Macro Puzzle`)
1. **Episode Goal**: What `A1` wants to achieve *in this episode* (smaller than the series goal).
2. **Episode Obstacle**: What blocks `A1` in this episode.
3. **One Main Shift**: By episode end, the situation or knowledge must irreversibly change from the opening (**at least one thing cannot go back to how it was**: a relationship fractures, a secret cracks, someone leaves).
4. **One New Layer of the Master Secret**: Peels back **one layer only** (never exposing the whole secret early).
5. **4-Stage Episode Storytelling Symphony (`Weaving the 5 Scene Structures Into a Complete Episode`)**:
   - **Open/Hook (`15–20%`, Scenes 1–2)**: Deploy **Opening Hook (`In Medias Res` + Anomaly)** + **Storytelling / `V.O.` Reflection** to immerse the viewer and plant the central question.
   - **Develop & Escalate (`40–50%`, Middle Scenes)**: Alternate **Dialogue / Confrontation Scenes** with **Clue Discovery / Micro-Visual Insert Scenes** (`Compress ↔ Release` contrast; never stack 3–4 identical scene types in a row).
   - **Climax (`20–25%`, Peak `10s–15s` Scenes)**: Detonate accumulated tension via a **`15.0s` Multi-Turn Dramatic/Sitcom Showdown (`@Director`)** or a **`15.0s` High-Velocity Action/Chase/Combat Peak (`@CombatDirector`)**.
   - **Close/Cliffhanger (`10%`, Final 1–2 Scenes)**: Settle the emotional aftershock with a quiet **Tactile/Narrative Beat**, then pivot on an irreversible **Twist / Cliffhanger** that hands the baton directly into the next episode.
6. **One Memorable Visual/Prop Detail**: Used as the connector in Tier 3.

### 3. Tier 3 — Inter-Episode Links & Tracking Table (`Bảng Theo Dõi Tập`)
Every episode must deploy **at least two of the four inter-episode connectors**:
1. **Cliffhanger**: Ends the episode on an unanswered question or unresolved danger (*write the cliffhanger BEFORE writing the next episode, and open the next episode with its direct consequence*).
2. **Setup (`Mồi`) & Payoff (`Trả`)**: Plant a detail in Episode $n$, pay it off in Episode $n+2$ or later. Every setup must be paid off; every payoff must be planted in advance.
3. **Visible Consequence (`Hậu quả`)**: An action in Episode $n$ causes an observable consequence in Episode $n+1$.
4. **Recurring Motif (`Motif lặp`)**: An image, sound, prop (such as the 1984 brass truck keyring), or echo line that returns with a shifted meaning each time.

### 4. Tier 4 — Per-Scene Rough Prose, 5 Scene Action Structures & Intent-First Routing (`@Director 10s/15s` vs. `@CombatDirector 15s`)
- **Scene Allocation inside an Episode (~3 minutes = `12–18 scenes`)**:
  * **Default `10.0s` Scene (Opening Hooks, Storytelling/`V.O.`, Standard Dialogue, Confrontation & Clue Twists — Routed to `@Director` `[🎭 MODEL TIÊU CHUẨN · 10.0s · 1–4 Shots]`)**: **1 dramatic beat**, `3–5` sentences of rough prose structured by the **Scene Type** (*Opening Hook*, *Storytelling/V.O.*, *Dialogue/Confrontation*, or *Clue Discovery*), **`0–2` spoken sentences (`0–22 words total`, `0 spoken words` in the final settle beat)**.
  * **Extended `15.0s` Dialogue / Sitcom / Climax Showdown Scene (`@Director` `[🎭 MODEL TIÊU CHUẨN · 15.0s · 3–4 Shots]`)**: `4–6` sentences of continuous rough prose for multi-turn banter or high-voltage boardroom/family showdowns (`2–3` turns, `18–34 words total`).
  * **High-Velocity `15.0s` Action / Chase / Combat Scene (Routed Exclusively to `@CombatDirector` `[⚡ MODEL KHUYẾN NGHỊ: SEEDANCE · 15.0s · 4–5 Shots]`)**: `5–7` sentences of kinetic rough prose structured around the **Kinetic Contrast Flow (`Tension Ignition / Snap Freeze ➔ Full-Speed Flight/Pursuit [0 dialogue] ➔ Hard Stop + VFX/Trap [0 dialogue] ➔ Dominance / Checkmate Line ➔ Terminal Fallout [0 dialogue]`)**.
  * **One Job per Scene & Rhythmic Variety**: Each scene does **strictly ONE thing**. Never place three consecutive scenes with the same scene structure, emotion, or tempo.
- **Step-by-Step Scene Drafting Rule**:
  1. **First, write the 1-Line Scene List (`Danh sách cảnh thô 1 dòng`)**: For every scene, write 1 line: `Scene # [10s/15s @Director or 15s @CombatDirector | Scene Type: Hook / V.O. / Dialogue / Clue / Action | Đạo cụ Tier-1: <prop-*.jpg / Không>] | Location, who is present, what they do, for what purpose`.
  2. **Second, write the Continuous Rough Prose (`Văn xuôi liền mạch, không chia giây`)** for each scene covering: **(a) Scene Purpose, Scene Action Structure & Recommended Shot Rhythm (flexing by beat and line length)**, **(b) Location, Characters Present (`<char_ref.jpg>`), Time (+ `TH1/TH2/TH3` & `10s/15s @Director / 15s @CombatDirector` tag)**, **(c) Tier-1 Prop / Weapon Reference (`prop-*.jpg` $\to$ `<<<Image X>>>`), `00:00` Hand-Grip or Surface Placement, and Close-Up Detail**, **(d) Observable Action & Micro-Visual Detail in Present Tense (`Image Speaks First`)**, **(e) Spoken / `V.O.` Dialogue (`0–22w` for `10s`, `18–34w` for `15s` dialogue, using the Single Vocal-Timbre + Tone Tag `Cách 1`)**, and **(f) Closing Hook Point (`Điểm chốt` leading into the next scene)**.

### 5. Tier 5 — Inter-Scene Links (`Quy Tắc Nối Cảnh & Nối Thoại`)
1. **Causality Over Listing (`"Vì vậy / Nhưng"` — Ban `"Và rồi"`)**: Every pair of scenes must connect via **Therefore (`Vì vậy`)** or **But (`Nhưng`)**. If they only connect via *"And then (`Và rồi`)"*, the scene is redundant.
2. **Action-Reaction Chain**: Scene $K$ ends with an action, question, or emotional strike; Scene $K+1$ opens with its immediate reaction or consequence.
3. **Visual, Acoustic, or Word Bridge**: An object, sound, or final keyword (`Word Hook`) at the end of Scene $K$ echoes at the start of Scene $K+1$ (e.g., brass keyring tapping mahogany wood $\to$ oak door latch clicking open).
4. **Alternating Emotional & Vocal Rhythm**: Alternate tense/dense scenes with quiet/sparse or silent scenes (`Model 0`).
5. **Visual Spatial/Temporal Cue**: If jumping in location or time (`TH2`), show it immediately via a visual detail in the first second.
6. **Chekhov's Detail Payoff**: Any prop or spoken line emphasized in Scene $K$ must have a concrete effect in a subsequent scene.

### 6. The Continuity & Dialogue Bible (`Sổ Liên Tục & Sổ Thoại` — Updated After Every Episode)
To prevent character, prop, and knowledge drift across AI video prompts, maintain a unified **Continuity & Dialogue Bible**:
1. **Character Cards & Voice Profiles**: Proper Name (`Bradley`, `Harold`, `Elena`, `Dante`), outfit reference image per episode/context (`-home`, `-work`, `-date`, `-outing`), identifying traits, **Voice Profile (`Rhythm, Vocabulary, Kinship Register Card, What They Never Say Straight, Fixed AI Voice Spec`)**.
2. **Location & Prop Cards**: Tag, reference image, fixed layout/seating map, and current prop custody (`where the prop is and who holds it right now`).
3. **Post-Episode State & Dialogue Ledger (`Nhật ký trạng thái & Sổ thoại sau mỗi tập`)**:
   - **Who Knows What (`Ai biết gì`)**: Exact information state of each character at the end of Episode $N$ so no character reacts to unlearned information in Episode $N+1$.
   - **Dialogue Hooks**: **Echo Lines (`Câu lặp` + target payoff episode)**, **Promises & Threats**, **Spoken Lies** (who lied to whom), and **Unfinished Lines**.
   - **Physical & Relational State**: Prop location, wardrobe/injury changes, and kinship pronoun shifts (`e.g., from "Bố" to "Ông"`).

### 7. The Canonical 7-Step Episode Workflow (Stage 3 Execution Order)
1. Read the **Inter-Episode Tracking Table** & **Continuity/Dialogue Ledger** (`Cliffhanger`, `Setups`, `Who Knows What` from Episode $N-1$).
2. Lock **Episode Goal, Obstacle, Main Irreversible Shift, and 1 New Secret Layer** for Episode $N$.
3. Draft the **1-Line Scene List (`12–18 scenes`)**, weave the **5 Scene Action Structures** (`Opening Hook`, `Storytelling/V.O.`, `Dialogue`, `Clue Discovery`, `Action`) across the **4-Stage Episode Symphony** (`15–20% / 40–50% / 20–25% / 10%`), and assign `10s / 15s` (`@Director` for narrative/dialogue/sitcom/showdown) vs. `15s` (`@CombatDirector` for high-velocity action/chase/combat).
4. Verify every inter-scene transition with **`"Vì vậy (Therefore)"` / `"Nhưng (But)"`**.
5. Write the **Continuous Present-Tense Rough Prose & Dialogue** for each scene matching its Scene Action Structure (`10s = 0–22w`, `15s Dialogue = 18–34w`, `15s Action = 0–16w`).
6. Update the **Tracking Table & Continuity/Dialogue Ledger** at the end of the episode and present `✋ CP3`.
7. After user approval at `CP3`, hand over to `@Director` (`10s` & `15s` dialogue/drama/sitcom scenes) and `@CombatDirector` (`15s` action/chase/combat scenes) at Stage 4 to compile the scene-by-scene **Unified Multi-Shot Storyboard Prompts**.
