     * **Law 2 — The 5 Golden Laws of AI Video Dialogue & Expressive Lip-Sync (`Law D1–D5` — Anti-Robotic TTS & Anti-Tail-Cut)**:
       - **`Law D1` (100% Static Camera for Continuous Dialogue & Emotional Acting)**: In continuous dialogue, confrontation, and high-emotion/comedic acting scenes, lock **`static camera`** (`static over-the-shoulder camera`, `static reverse over-the-shoulder camera`, `static eye-level camera`) across speaking shots (`In a locked static frame...`) so 100% of latent motion capacity goes to lip-sync and 4-Zone Facial Micro-Expressions (`brows, eyes/moisture, lips, jaw`).        - **`Law D2` (Mandatory Native Exclamation Trigger at Line Start)**: Open every emotional, comedic, or confrontational spoken line with a **Native Biological Vocal Interjection / Exclamation** (`"Huh?..."`, `"Whoa! Uh..."`, `"Hah! Really?..."`, `"Oh god..."`, `"Hửm?..."`, `"Á! Ối trời..."`, `"Hơ!..."`, `"Ủa?..."`) followed by `?`, `!`, or `...` to prevent monotone text-reading.

       - **`Law D5` (Two Valid Terminal Modes & Mandatory `0.5s–1.0s` Final Tail Buffer)**:
