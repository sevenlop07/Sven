### 1.2 The 8 Non-Negotiable Laws of High-Density (`5–9 Shot`) Rapid-Cut Directing

Conversely, why do **6–9 separate `Shot N` cuts (`1.5s–2.0s / shot`)** render with razor-sharp Hollywood perfection? Because each shot gives the AI **1 clean camera angle + 1 single micro-strike sentence (`12–22 words`)**:
1. **Law 1 — Single `<<<Image X>>>` Declaration & 100% Pre-Declared Prop Inventory (`Zero Ghost Objects`)**: Declare `<<<Image 1>>>`, `<<<Image 2>>>`, `<<<Image 3>>>`, `<<<Image 4>>>` **ONLY ONCE** inside `[context]:`, `[subject]:`, and `[prop]:`. 4. **Law 4 — The `1.5s–2.0s` Micro-Strike Sentence Law (`1 Single Crisp Sentence of 12–22 Words per Rapid Shot`)**:
   - In a `1.5s–2.0s` rapid cut, write **strictly 1 single punchy natural sentence (`12–22 words`) executing 1 single physical micro-strike (`1 verb + 1 immediate physical impact/splash/spark`)** — zero `→` arrows, zero multi-step compounding inside one short shot, and zero static repetition from `[subject]:`.
   - Jump camera scale and angle by **> 45°** on every consecutive cut (`Extreme Close-Up Side ➔ Tight Overhead ➔ Low-Angle Close-Up ➔ Macro Diagonal ➔ Whip-Tilt MCU`).
5. **Law 5 — Zero Speech During `1.5s–2.5s` Rapid Cuts & Zero Tail-Cut Law (`0 Spoken Words` in Final Shot)**:
   - Keep all `1.5s–2.5s` rapid action/craft/combat cuts **100% wordless (`0 spoken words`)**.
