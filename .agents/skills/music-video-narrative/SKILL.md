## ⏱️ § 2. MATHEMATICAL TIME ALLOCATION & MUSICAL MAPPING

The total track duration ($T$ seconds) is strictly partitioned according to the Hollywood 6-Beat mathematical standard:

$$\text{Total Duration } T = B_1(3\%) + B_2(12\%) + B_3(35\%) + B_4(30\%) + B_5(12\%) + B_6(8\%)$$

### 🎼 6-Beat & Musical Structure Synchronization Matrix

| Beat | % | Exact Duration | Musical Section | Epic Cinematic Narrative & Lyrical Function | Cast Dynamic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Beat 1: HOOK** | **3%** | $\approx T \times 0.03$ | **Intro / First Bars** | **Visual Paradox & Frozen Kinetic Potential**: Impossible visual phenomenon (e.g., a colossal sword suspended mid-fall above an inverted sea) before the musical storm ignites. | **Solo / Monumental Prop Focus** |
| **Beat 2: SETUP** | **12%** | $\approx T \times 0.12$ | **Verse 1** | **Old World Equilibrium & Generational Trauma**: Ancient cursed empire / broken oath / shattered titan. Plant the **Wound Prop** in its trapped/bound physical state. | **Tier A vs. Cursed Past** (Ancestral memory / Phantom) |
| **Beat 3: DRAMA** | **35%** | $\approx T \times 0.35$ | **Pre-Chorus 1 ➔ Chorus 1 ➔ Verse 2 ➔ Pre-Chorus 2** | **Escalating Ideological Battle & Sealed Exits**: Protagonist wages war against inevitable destiny or formidable Adversary (Tier B1). **≥60% Dramatic Confrontation**. | **Beat 6: ECHO** | **8%** | $\approx T \times 0.08$ | **Outro / Final Fade** | **Transformed Universe & 180° Echo Prop Payoff**: Cosmic horizon heals. Wound Prop transforms 180° into eternal light. Standalone philosophical maxim (<15 words). | **Tier A + Tier C Transformed Cosmos** |

## 💡 § 3. THE 3 ELITE DYNAMIC UPGRADES AT BLOCKBUSTER SCALE

### 1. 🔥 The Thermal Polarity Law (Luật Đảo Cực Nhiệt Độ Cảm Xúc)
- **Beat 4.3 (Peak Musical Drop / All Hope Is Lost)**:
  - *Lighting & Visuals*: Harsh desaturated **5600K Monochrome Cold** (void vortex, shattering black glacier, falling meteor ash, raging blizzard extinguishing all flame).
  - *Acoustics & Biomechanics*: Crashing dissonant instruments; protagonist's chest heaving, pupils dilated in terror, bloodless lips trembling as their weapon crumbles to dust.
- **Beat 5.1 (Safe Harbor / Drop to Acoustic)**:
  - *Lighting & Visuals*: Abrupt thermal snap to **2700K Soft Celestial Gold** (solar flare breaking through eclipse, golden magma turning into fertile soil, warm divine fire).
  - *Acoustics & Rhythm*: Full silence **`[pause: 1.5s]`** before acoustic notes and warm whisper vocals penetrate the quiet.

### 2. 👥 Cast Dynamic Rotation Law (Luật Xoay Vòng Trọng Tâm Nhân Vật)
- **Beat 1**: Solo hero / Close-up of monumental paradoxical artifact.
- **Beat 2**: Protagonist (Tier A) confronting the colossal phantom of ancestral trauma.
- **Beat 3**: Protagonist (Tier A) in high-stakes ideological clash against Nemesis (Tier B1).
- **Beat 4**: Protagonist (Tier A) stranded alone amidst the cosmic debris of their destroyed world.
- **Beat 5**: Protagonist (Tier A) lifted from the abyss by the unconditional grace of Companion/Guide (Tier B2).
- **Beat 6**: Protagonist (Tier A) standing atop the new world, joined by the resurrected civilization (Tier C).

### 3. 🪞 The Dual-Prop Transformation System (Hệ Thống Đạo Cụ Chuyển Hóa Kép)
- **Wound Prop (Beat 2 / Verse 1)**: Traumatic, bound, decaying artifact representing the central curse or fear of the lyrics (e.g., an obsidian blade chained to a crumbling titan skull).
- **Echo Prop (Beat 6 / Outro)**: 180° Physical Metamorphosis into a vessel of cosmic rebirth (e.g., the blade melts into liquid starlight, blooming into a colossal golden World Tree).
