## 🏛️ CORE SCREENWRITING ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM RULES

### 0. The Primacy of Human Emotion & The Universal Transformation Arc
* **PEOPLE OVER PROPS (LINH HỒN LÀ CON NGƯỜI — ĐẠO CỤ CHỈ LÀ XÚC TÁC PHỤ TRỢ)**:
  - Cinema is the crucible of the human soul under pressure. Props are NEVER the story; they are minor sensory anchors.
  - A story revolving around an object is shallow gimmickry. A masterclass script revolves around childhood trauma, moral dilemma, toxic ego, and spiritual metamorphosis. The prop merely reflects the actor's internal agony.
* **THE PROTAGONIST GRAVITATIONAL LAW (TRỌNG TÂM TUYỆT ĐỐI VÀO NHÂN VẬT CHÍNH — CẤM NẠN NHÂN THỤ ĐỘNG — Doctrine §1.1)**:
  - The protagonist (A1) is the sole gravitational engine of the narrative. The plot MUST revolve 100% around A1's active choices, burning desire, and internal dilemma.
  - **Strict Ban on Passive Victims**: A1 is NEVER a helpless punching bag who merely weeps, suffers, or endures while secondary characters make all the discoveries and drive the plot. In EVERY scene, A1 pursues an active want and executes a concrete tactic.
  - **Protagonist-Driven Causality**: The escalating crisis is caused directly by A1's flawed choices under pressure (powered by their Lie). Secondary characters exist to test A1's wound and force A1's ultimate moral transformation.
* **LIBERATION FROM RIGID CHECKLISTS (PHÁ BỎ TÍNH CỤC BỘ & MÁY MÓC)**:
  - Formulas (6-beat, 8-beat, or 13-beat) are merely external pacing rulers. The true universal engine of narrative power is the **6-Stage Internal Psychological Metamorphosis Arc**:
    1. *The Protected Lie (Bản ngã & Vỏ bọc an toàn giả tạo)*
    2. *The Irreversible Disruption (Cú sốc xé toạc vùng an toàn)*
    3. *The Crucible & False Resistance (Lò luyện thử thách & Nỗ lực chống cự bằng thói quen cũ)*
    4. *The Abyss & Ego Death (Đáy vực tuyệt lộ & Cái chết của Bản ngã)*
    5. *Anagnorisis & Moral Epiphany (Khoảnh Khắc Bừng Tỉnh Chân Lý & Sự tha thứ/chuộc tội)*
    6. *Transcendence & Restorative Catharsis (Siêu việt hóa & Tái sinh nhân cách mới)*

#### A. Format A: Standalone Short Film (60s–120s) — The Invariant 10-Second Scene Atom Mapping
> [!CAUTION]
> **INVARIANT LAW: ZERO FRACTIONAL SECONDS — EVERY SCENE IS EXACTLY 10.0 SECONDS**.
> Total duration $T = N \times 10.0$s. All dramatic beats map to **WHOLE 10-SECOND SCENE ATOMS**:
> - **Standard 60s (6 Scenes × 10s = 60s)**:
>   • `Scene 01 [00:00–00:10]` (10s): **Beat 1 (Hook)** — Visual Paradox & Cognitive Gap (3s hook).
>   • `Scene 02 [00:10–00:20]` (10s): **Beat 2 (Setup)** — Root Wound & Wound Prop Plant.
>   • `Scene 03 [00:20–00:30]` (10s): **Beat 3A (Drama 1)** — Flawed Tactics & Ideological Clash (≥60% Dialogue).
>   • `Scene 04 [00:30–00:40]` (10s): **Beat 3B (Drama 2)** — Near-Miss Obstruction & Sealed Exits.
>   • `Scene 05 [00:40–00:50]` (10s): **Beat 4 (Climax)** — 3-Wave Truth Bomb ➔ Rock Bottom ➔ Ego Death (Peak 10/10).
>   • `Scene 06 [00:50–01:00]` (10s): **Beat 5 & 6 (Release & Echo)** — Thermal Shock, 180° Echo Prop Payoff & Closing Image (no spoken moral, Doctrine §8.8).
> - **Standard 120s (12 Scenes × 10s = 120s / 2m00s)**:
>   • `Scene 01` (10s): Beat 1 (Hook) | `Scenes 02–03` (20s): Beat 2 (Setup) | `Scenes 04–07` (40s): Beat 3 (Drama, ≥60% Dialogue) | `Scenes 08–10` (30s): Beat 4 (Climax Peak 10/10) | `Scene 11` (10s): Beat 5 (Release) | `Scene 12` (10s): Beat  - **12 eps**: Ep 1, 2 (Act 1) | Ep 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 (Act 2 · Midpoint Ep 6) | Ep 8, 9, 10, 11 (Act 3) | Ep 12 (Act 4).
  - **16 eps**: Ep 1, 2, 3 (Act 1) | Ep 4–10 (Act 2 · Midpoint Ep 8) | Ep 11–14 (Act 3) | Ep 15–16 (Act 4).
  - **20 eps**: Ep 1–4 (Act 1) | Ep 5–13 (Act 2 · Midpoint Ep 11) | Ep 14–18 (Act 3) | Ep 19–20 (Act 4).

* **Episodic Mandates by Act**:
  - `Act 1 (Inception & Trap)`: Establish root wound & contract trap; **FORBIDDEN: Early victory, premature reconciliation, or cathartic release**. Ends on Cliffhanger C1/C2.
  - `Act 2 (Escalation & Midpoint)`: Stakes escalate, betrayal strikes (`TW-05 Judas Kiss`), Midpoint Twist shatters false hope. Ends on Cliffhanger C3/C4.
  - `Act 3 (Grand Climax)`: Dark Night of the Soul ➔ The 8-Beat High-Intensity Showdown ➔ Unmerited Grace (`Stunned Mercy / 10`). Ends on Cliffhanger C5/C6.
  - `Act 4 (Restorative Grace)`: 100% resolution, 180° Echo Prop payoff, community reconciliation, positive ending. **ZERO CLIFFHANGER**.

* **Anti-Sitcom / Anti-Vignette Continuum Law**: A multi-episode series is NEVER a sequence of disconnected "Problem-of-the-Week" sketches where characters fight over an arbitrary folk myth and make up 100 seconds later. Characters possess unbroken psychological continuity: their relationships permanently alter at each checkpoint, and core emotional wounds escalate across acts rather than resetting.

* **Anti-Stiffness & Modal Particle Law (Luật Chống Thoại Cộc Lốc & Bắt Buộc Trợ Từ Tình Thái)**:
  - Spoken dialogue in dramatic domestic conflict MUST NEVER be written as curt, robotic, or literary declarations devoid of exclamations and emotional particles (e.g., *"Con tự lo được... má đừng bận lòng thêm"* or *"Tay mầy... mầy đi truyền nước biển hồi nào sao hổng nói tao hay?!"*).
  - Screenwriters MUST inject authentic regional exclamations (*Trời ơi, Trời đất quỷ thần ơi, Mèn đéc ơi, Má ơi con lạy má, Anh ơi...*) and sentence-ending modal particles (*...hả trời, ...hả mầy, ...nè má, ...nè con, ...chớ bộ, ...đâu hà, ...nghen con, ...hết trơn, ...đặng*) while strictly respecting the `<= 18 words` hard cap.
  - **Strict Cultural Taboo Ban**: NEVER use death-wishing or gloating slang like *"Chết chưa"* or *"Đáng đời"* when a family member collapses or is sick. Real maternal panic uses immediate protective shouts: *"Duyên ơi coi chừng té! Khang ơi, vô đỡ vợ mầy lẹ lên con!"*

* **The Two-Tier Action Framework & Mandatory Directorial Cadence Handshake (Doctrine §3.3)**:
  - Every scene authored by the Screenwriter MUST orchestrate Action across two tiers: **[Tier 1: Relationship] + [Tier 2: 10s Dynamic]**, declaring its CADENCE: and ACTION DYNAMIC: in the Scene Intent Card or Scene Header to explicitly instruct @Director on camera pacing and eradicate mechanical 3-shot defaults:
    * **Dạng 1: High-Velocity Stichomythia (CADENCE: RAPID_SPARRING)**: High-tempo verbal clash, rapid volley exchanges, zero idle buffer. Directs Director to use Track B 2-Shot (5s/5s) or 3-Phase rapid cuts along 180° axis. STRICTLY FORBIDS 3-second silent reaction shots.
    * **Dạng 2: Single-Speaker Dominance (CADENCE: ASYMMETRIC_DOMINANCE)**: Asymmetrical power clash. One character speaks continuously across 1–2 sequential volleys (20–30 words) while the listener is trapped in silent physical defense.
    * **Dạng 3: The Somatic Aftershock (CADENCE: TRUTH_BOMB_SHOCK)**: Devastating revelation (Truth Bomb) where dialogue terminates by 00:04.5–00:05.0. Directs Director to use Track B 3-Shot where the second half is a mandatory 3.5s–5.0s silent collapse reaction.
    * **Dạng 4: Pure Covert Action (CADENCE: PURE_VISUAL_STEALTH)**: Covert, tactical, or investigative action. Spoken dialogue = 0 words. Directs Director to use Track A continuous take or macro insert.
    * **Dạng 5: False Dawn to Cold Water (CADENCE: COLD_WATER_CLIFFHANGER)**: False Dawn (00:00–00:07.5) shattered by brutal complication (00:07.5–00:10.0). Directs Director to use Track AB snap-cut to black.
    *(Note: For subdued vulnerability, CADENCE: INTIMATE_CONFESSION may be used as a Track A slow push-in variant).*

* **Breaking the Dual-Turn Dialogue Trap (Asymmetrical Combat & Single-Speaker Dominance)**:
  - Screenwriters must eliminate the mechanical impulse to write every scene as an alternating 1-line-A / 1-line-B tennis match:
    * *Single-Speaker Dominance (20–30 words)*: In aggressive confrontations or interrogations, one character speaks continuously across 1–2 sequential volleys while the listener is trapped in silent physical defense.
    * *Interruption & Stichomythia*: Rapid overlapping sentences where Speaker B cuts Speaker A off mid-sentence.
    * *Physical Resistance Over Verbal Ping-Pong*: The listener parries an accusation with an immediate physical action (ripping a paper, turning away, slamming a drawer) rather than a verbal reply.

### 1B. The Universal 3-Level Scriptwriting Continuity Engine (Hệ Thống Móc Xích Kịch Bản Tổng Quát 3 Tầng)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ANTI-FRAGMENTATION MANDATE: COHESION ACROSS SCENES & EPISODES**:
> A cinematic film is NEVER a disjointed sequence of standalone clips. The Screenwriter MUST engineer unbreakable continuity across three nested architectural tiers:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE UNIVERSAL 3-LEVEL SCRIPTWRITING CONTINUITY ENGINE                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIER 1: MACRO LEVEL (SEASON & SERIES ARC) — THE IRREVERSIBLE ESCALATION LADDER                        │
│ • Symmetrical Character Transformation: Wound (Ep 1) ➔ False Armor ➔ Ego Death ➔ Catharsis (Finale).  │
│ • Irreversible Stakes: Each episode escalates conflict to an irreversible tier. Zero status quo reset.  │
│ • Point of No Return: Every episode must conclude with an action that permanently alters reality.      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIER 2: MESO LEVEL (BETWEEN EPISODES) — THE BROKEN MESO-LOOP & CLIFFHANGER ANATOMY                    │
│ • The Invariant Binge Law: Ending (Beat 5–6) of Episode N ≡ Detonation Trigger (Beat 1) of Episode N+1.│
│ • The 3-Second Cold Water Slap: False Dawn in final 10s shattered at 00:09 by a ruthless complication. │
│ • Zero Temporal Gap (Real-Time Handover): Terminal frame at 00:10 of Ep N IS opening frame at 00:00    │
│   of Ep N+1. Same physical momentum, lighting, coordinates, and bio-respiration.                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIER 3: MICRO LEVEL (BETWEEN 10.0s SCENE ATOMS) — THE "THEREFORE / BUT" CAUSALITY CHAIN                │
│ • The Anti-"And Then" Law: Scenes NEVER link by "And then...". Every scene link MUST BE:               │
│   - "THEREFORE..." (Action in Scene N creates mandatory kinetic/emotional consequence in Scene N+1) OR│
│   - "BUT..." (Action in Scene N strikes an unexpected physical/psychological obstruction in Scene N+1)│
│ • The Scene-to-Scene Handoff Triangle:                                                                 │
│   1. Kinetic Vector & Spatial Anchor: Momentum direction at 00:10 seamlessly continues at 00:00.      │
│   2. Tactile Prop Bridge: Interactive prop held in Scene N becomes active catalyst in Scene N+1.       │
│   3. Keyword & Emotional Handoff: Spoken dialogue in Scene N+1 directly retorts a keyword from Scene N.│
│ • Micro-Loop Overlap: Terminal 1.5s of Scene N seeds an unresolved micro-hook triggering Scene N+1.    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 1. Tier 1 (Macro): The Irreversible Escalation Ladder Across Episodes
In a multi-episode narrative (4 to 20 episodes), each episode occupies an irreversible tier of conflict. A script that repeats the same level of stakes is dead:
1. **Tier 1 (Personal Survival & Inciting Trap)**: Direct physical/financial need. The protagonist accepts a dangerous compromise to survive.
2. **Tier 2 (Relational Crisis & Betrayal)**: The compromise drags loved ones into mortal danger. Subordinate satellites cross lines (`TW-05 Judas Kiss`).
3. **Tier 3 (Institutional / Communal Isolation)**: Society, law, or public mob (Tier C) turns aggressively against the protagonist. Exits sealed.
4. **Tier 4 (Existential Crucible & Ego Death)**: The false armor crumbles completely. Total rock bottom (`PEAK 10/10`). Discovery of the devastating truth.
5. **Tier 5 (Transcendent Grace & Communal Restoration)**: Resolution achieved through moral sacrifice and unconditional pardon, inspiring collective healing.

#### 2. Tier 2 (Meso): The Inter-Episode Binge Loop Engine
To eliminate episode-to-episode fragmentation and engineer addictive binge retention:
- **The False Dawn to Cold Water Slap**:
  - `00:00–00:07.5` of Episode N's Final Scene: Hero achieves temporary success or breathes a sigh of relief (False Dawn).
  - `00:07.5–00:09.5`: Hard shock disruption strikes (Cold Water Slap: an arrest warrant slammed onto table, a fire alarm screaming, a weapon cocked behind the door).
  - `00:09.5–00:10.0`: Immediate cut to black on the hero's paralyzed, breathless face.
- **Zero Temporal Gap Standard**:
  - Episode N+1 does NOT begin "the next morning" or "meanwhile elsewhere".
  - Episode N+1 Scene 1 opens at the exact microsecond and camera angle where Episode N froze: the shock wave traveling, the hand trembling on the door handle, the breath caught in the throat.

#### 3. Tier 3 (Micro): The Intra-Episode "Therefore / But" Causality Matrix
Within any single episode, 10-second Scene Atoms must be forged together through relentless causality:
- **Rule of Causality**:
  - ❌ *Banned ("And then")*: Scene 1 shows hero baking bread. Scene 2 shows hero walking in street. Scene 3 shows hero talking to merchant. (Disjointed slideshow).
  - ✅ *Mandatory ("Therefore / But")*: Scene 1 flour jar falls empty, mother faints; **THEREFORE** Scene 2 hero sprints out the door grabbing father's relic; **BUT** Scene 3 street elders spot the relic and shout accusations; **THEREFORE** Scene 4 hero is cornered against the storehouse wall...
- **The Scene Handoff Triangle Execution**:
  - *Vector Continuity*: If character exits frame-right at Scene N, camera must establish entry from frame-left at Scene N+1.
  - *Prop Continuity*: The object touched at 00:09 of Scene N must be in physical contact at 00:00 of Scene N+1.
  - *Dialogue Keyword Handoff*: The core noun/verb of the previous volley must be seized and weaponized in the next volley (e.g., *"This debt will destroy you"* ➔ *"Then let it destroy me, because I will not yield!"*).

### 1C. The 6 Invariant Laws of Dialogue Continuity & Conversational Control (Gates 4B-4F Enforced)
* **Single Dramatic Spine (Mandatory Central Dramatic Question - CDQ)**: Every episode must track ONE unified emotional conflict across its 10s Scene Atoms. Zero disconnected sub-plots. $\mathbf{Spine} = \mathbf{Want} + \mathbf{Deadly\ Obstacle} + \mathbf{Ticking\ Clock\ Stakes}$. Any line not advancing the CDQ is banned waste.
* **The 4-Way Cross-Scene Dialogue Relay (Baton Protocol)**: Scenes NEVER connect via "And then...". Every scene's opening volley MUST be triggered by the previous scene across at least one vector:
  1. *Cause-and-Effect Chain*: An ultimatum or physical action at the end of Scene N directly forces an immediate reaction/retaliation at the start of Scene N+1.
  2. *Keyword Echo & Semantic Inversion*: Seize the most sensitive keyword from Scene N's tail and weaponize/invert its meaning in Scene N+1's head (e.g., *"Má sắp **chết**..."* ➔ *"**Chết**? Tao mới là thằng chết trước chiều nay nếu không có tiền!"*).
  3. *Emotional Inertia & Acoustic Aftershock*: Retain the physiological momentum (hyperventilation, choked swallowing, trembling breath) across the cut. Never reset to calm speech after a rupture.
  4. *Prop & Spatial Anchor Relay*: An interactive Chekhov prop handled in Scene N becomes the immediate focus of friction in Scene N+1.
* **Dialogue as Tactical Action (Subtext-Over-Exposition)**:
  - Every line must be governed by an internal tactical action verb: `[ATTACK]`, `[DEFEND]`, `[PROBE]`, `[BARGAIN]`, `[TRAP]`, or `[DESPERATE-STAND]`.
  - Zero expository chatter or reciting facts both characters already know. The spoken text is merely the tip of the iceberg; subtext carries the real lethal payload.
* **Law 1: The Action-Reaction Chain (Gate 4C, warning)**: In an exchange, Speaker 2 answers, counters, deflects, or is changed by Speaker 1's line or action. Two characters stating parallel positions is a debate, not a scene. Single-speaker modes (solo line + silent listener, direct monologue, inner V.O.) are valid when the Intent Card states why (Doctrine §4).
* **Law 2: The Keyword Handoff Relay (Gate 4C)**: The core noun/verb/concept of Scene N or Line 1 must be seized and weaponized in Scene N+1 or Line 2.
* **Law 3: The Pronoun Shift Ladder & Post-Reconciliation Protocol (Gate 4D)**:
  - *Setup*: Intimate Kinship (`Má - Con`, `Anh - Em`, `Mẹ - Con`).
  - *Friction*: Sarcastic Distance (`Tôi - Chị/Cô`, stripping family status).
  - *Climax*: Visceral Fury (`Tao - Mày`, primal territory defense).
  - *Resolution & Catharsis*: Reclaimed maternal kinship (`Má - Con`, `con Duyên`, `con dâu`).
  - **Post-Reconciliation Protocol (Project Rule, Gate 4D)**: Once reconciliation occurs, elders stop using alienating address terms. The scene number and the banned terms are declared per project in `output/<slug>/project_rules.json` (`kinship_ladder`); project example: from scene 6, Má Ba never uses `Chị`/`tui` and locks into `má - con`.
* **Law 4: The 18-Word Acoustic Breath Limit & Anti-Bookish Grammar (Gates 4B & 4F)**:
  - Hard cap <= 18 words per line — a ceiling, not a target. Most lines are far shorter, and lines shrink as stakes rise (Doctrine §3.4). Time each line with `0b-temporal-camera-grammar.md` §2.
  - Spoken lines must use authentic Southern particles (`nghen`, `dạ`, `hà`, `hén`, `thiệt`, `hổng`, `vầy`, `dữ nghen`).
  - Zero bookish written conjunctions (`bởi vì`, `cho nên`, `tuy nhiên`, `mặc dù`).
* **Law 5: The Domestic Prop & Biological Meal Continuity Standard (Gate 4E)**:
  - Continuous timeline scenes must maintain 100% biological and culinary continuity. A postpartum dish prepared in the kitchen (`canh bồ ngót thịt bằm`) must be the exact dish served and eaten in the bedroom.
* **Law 6: The Subtext-First Mandate & Anti-Declarative Melodrama (Doctrine §3.8)**:
  - **Zero Psychological Self-Diagnosis**: Characters under crisis never explain their wounds, trauma, or motives aloud like a clinical therapist.
  - **Zero Symmetrical Debate Volleys**: Dialogue must not be a polite ping-pong match of ideological talking points. Real human conflict is asymmetrical, jagged, and heavily reliant on actions, deflection, and somatic silence.
  - **Zero Cliché Faint Reversals**: A medical collapse or fainting fit must NEVER be used to instantly convert an antagonist from tyrant to saint in 10 seconds. Reversals must be forged through compounding friction and earned vulnerability across full acts.

### 1D. The Multi-Subject Continuous Presence Mandate (Quy Tắc Hiện Diện Liên Tục Đa Nhân Vật)
* **Zero Disappearing Characters**:
  - In any scene or room containing multiple characters (e.g. Mother and Daughter-in-law talking in the bedroom, then Son enters), existing characters MUST NOT mysteriously vanish or stop existing.
  - When character C enters, characters A and B remain physically present, emotionally reacting, and maintaining eyeline contact along the 180° action axis.
  - Every character present in the physical room must have their location, posture, listening reaction, and eyeline continuously accounted for until an explicit departure/exit occurs.

### 4. The False Dawn & Double-Bind Reversal Architecture (Công Thức Gáo Nước Lạnh)
* **The 4-Stage Cold Water Cycle**:
  1. *Stage 1: False Dawn* — Bait hope, generate dopamine surge.
  2. *Stage 2: Cold Water Slap* — Brutal rejection/betrayal converting dopamine into adrenaline shock.
  3. *Stage 3: Double-Bind* — Life-or-death moral trap (Truth = loved one dies; Lie = preserve life but endure disgrace).
  4. *Stage 4: Dramatic Irony Agony* — Audience weeps because they know the hidden truth while the hero remains in the dark.
* **The Noble Lie Law**: Character willingly wears the mask of a villain/traitor to push their loved one out of the line of fire. Physical micro-biomechanics betray spoken words (hidden tears, bleeding clenched knuckles).
* **The Multi-Episode Binge Loop**: $\mathbf{Ending\ (Beat\ 5	ext{--}6)\ of\ Ep\ N} \;\equiv\; \mathbf{Cold\ Water\ Slap\ (Beat\ 1)\ of\ Ep\ N+1}$.


#### A. The Near-Miss Mechanism & The Tantalus Torment (Cơ Chế Cú Suýt Soát & Sự Cản Trở Của Phản Diện)
* **The Tantalus Effect (Hiệu Ứng Nước Tới Miệng Lại Rút)**:
  - Never allow effortless discovery. When a character is 1 millimeter away from truth or reunion, a formidable barrier MUST intervene to trigger intense delayed gratification.
  - **The 3-Tier Escalation Ladder of Antagonist Obstruction**:
    1. *Tier 1: Tactical Foresight & Physical Sabotage* (Phản diện đi trước 1 bước: tiêu hủy chứng cứ, dời nhân chứng đi trước 5 phút).
    2. *Tier 2: Psychological Seduction & Poisoned Half-Truths* (Đòn ly gián: gieo sự thật nửa vời khiến hai bên tự nghi ngờ và từ chối gặp nhau).
    3. *Tier 3: Asymmetrical Hostage & Forced Moral Renunciation* (Bắt cóc đạo đức: ép nhân vật phải tự tay hủy bỏ cuộc gặp gỡ để bảo vệ người vô tội qua The Noble Lie).
  - **The Anti-Coincidence Law**: Absolutely ZERO random accidents (car crashes, slipping, sudden amnesia). Obstructions must flow from calculated antagonist strategy or the protagonist's own tragic flaw.

#### B. The 5 Jaw-Dropping Reversal Mechanisms (Aristotle: Surprising yet Inevitable)
* **Aristotle's Poetics Standard**: A true twist shatters expectations upon detonation, but 3 seconds later feels 100% inevitable and earned.
  1. *The Sacrificial Villain / The Noble Lie*: The apparent monster was secretly taking the bullets to protect the hero.
  2. *The Moral Paradigm Shift*: The "holy cause" or mentor was secretly the oppressor; the "enemy" was the victim.
  3. *The Pyrrhic Victory & Paradoxical Surrender*: Winning the physical trophy brings spiritual death; true victory requires voluntarily surrendering the prize.
  4. *The Arrogant Red Herring*: Weaponizing the audience's and antagonist's own genre arrogance against them.
  5. *The Impossible Moral Crucible*: A no-win dilemma resolved only through selfless moral transcendence.

### 6. The Master Cinematic Prop Architecture & 3 AI Video Iron Laws (Nghệ Thuật Đạo Cụ)
In masterclass cinema, **props are never background clutter or decorative furniture**. A prop is the **physical extension of the character's soul**:

1. **The 5 Universal Cinematic Functions of Props**:
   - `Function 1: Chekhov's Gun` — Introduced innocuously in Act 1 (Setup) ➔ Must inevitably detonate as the dramatic/physical resolution in Act 3/4.
   - `Function 2: The Wound Prop ➔ Echo Prop Transformation System` — Symmetrical 180° transformation: Symbol of debt/shame/trauma in Act 1 ➔ Re-forged/burned/liberated state in Act 4, visually certifying internal redemption.
   - `Function 3: Business Props & Psychological Subtext` — Physicalized internal conflict: pouring tea until it overflows (suppressed terror), wiping spectacles slowly (buying time to plot), clutching a hidden heirloom until knuckles bleed (The Noble Lie).
   - `Function 4: Status & Power Levers` — Who physically holds the prop (pen, corporate seal, heavy iron key, executive chair) holds conversational dominance. Knocking down or seizing the prop flips the power dynamic.
   - `Function 5: The MacGuffin vs. The Truth Bomb` — Objects fought over by factions to test human greed vs. virtue, or the decisive forensic catalyst that shatters deception.

2. **The 3 Iron Laws of Props in Generative AI Video**:
   - **Law 1: Single Focal Prop Law**: STRICTLY MAXIMUM 1 interactive prop per scene to prevent diffusion model finger warping and floating artifacts.
   - **Law 2: Simple Geometry Law**: Mandatory solid, clearly textured geometries with tactile wear (frayed leather ledger, wax-sealed envelope, tarnished heavy brass key, rustic ceramic tea bowl) — BAN on complex glassy/liquid/multi-gear objects.
   - **Law 3: Tactile Locking Syntax**: Prompts MUST declare anatomical contact mechanics (e.g., `"dirt-crusted thumb and scarred index finger press firmly into the worn leather edge, knuckles blanching with physical tension"`).

3. **The Prop Mutability Lifecycle & Mandatory Entity Re-Naming Law (Quy Luật Đổi Tên Thực Thể Sau Phá Hủy)**:
   - **The 3-Phase Lifecycle**:
     - *Phase A: Intact Canonical State (Trạng thái Nguyên bản)*: e.g., `The Ledger`, `The Porcelain Teacup`.
     - *Phase B: Kinetic Fracture / Destruction Event (Thời điểm Phá hủy)*: e.g., `pages tearing apart into strips`, `ceramic shattering violently upon flagstone floor`.
     - *Phase C: Transformed Fragment State (Trạng thái Vụn vỡ Sau Phá hủy)*: Object is no longer intact; its physical entity has permanently fractured into fragments.
   - **MANDATORY ENTITY RE-NAMING LAW FOR STRUCTURAL FRACTURES**:
     - When an object undergoes structural destruction (torn, shattered, incinerated, crushed), prompts MUST permanently re-name the object to its new physical fragments (`The Ledger` ➔ `Torn Parchment Shreds`, `The Porcelain Cup` ➔ `Jagged Ceramic Shards`, `The Secret Decree` ➔ `Smoldering Ash Flakes`).
     - Retaining the intact noun (`The book falls`) causes generative diffusion models to hallucinate an intact book.
     - **Surface Alterations vs Structural Fractures**:
       - *Surface Alterations* (`soot-stained sleeve`, `mud-splattered ledger cover`): Retain canonical entity noun + surface texture adjectives.
       - *Structural Fractures* (torn, broken, shattered, burnt): BẮT BUỘC ĐỔI TÊN THÀNH THỰC THỂ MẢNH VỠ MỚI.

4. **Minimalist Prop Law (Luật Tối Giản Đạo Cụ)**:
   - Strictly 1–2 Primary Conflict Anchor Props per 100s episode. Zero background clutter.
   - Ambient tools or setup props (hex wrench, tea cups) must be permanently cleared to stationary rest immediately once their single functional beat is finished.

5. **Stationary Spatial Anchor Law for Heavy Fixtures**:
   - Heavy furniture, fixtures, and major architectural elements (Pine Crib, Dining Table, Bed, Altar) are permanently locked to their designated room/space coordinates across all scenes.
   - Heavy fixtures CANNOT teleport between rooms across scenes (e.g. pine crib in bedroom stays in bedroom across all 10 scenes).

6. **Anti-Ghost Prop Law & Single Instance Lock (Chống Nhân Bản Đạo Cụ)**:
   - In generative diffusion models (Kling, Runway, Sora), every active prop exists as strictly **ONE physical instance**.
   - **Explicit Resting Surface Clearance**: When a prop is lifted/moved from a surface, the screenplay and prompt MUST explicitly declare that the resting surface is clear (e.g., `leaving the tiled floor completely clear`), preventing diffusion text encoders from rendering duplicate ghost props.
   - **Master Prop Continuity Ledger**: Screenwriter and Director must maintain an unbroken chain-of-custody log from Scene 01 to Scene N.

### 8. Absolute Humanitarian Mandate: Positive Ending & Zero-Fatality Law
* **Zero Killings / Zero Executions / Zero Bloodshed as a Solution**:
  - Violence is strictly rejected as a moral resolution.
  - A2 faces accountability through **Moral Epiphany (Anagnorisis), legal disarmament, and restorative restitution**.
