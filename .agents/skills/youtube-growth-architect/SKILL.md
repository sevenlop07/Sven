## STEP 1 — KEYWORD-DRIVEN NICHE DISCOVERY & VALIDATION

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Golden Law of Niche Validation**: Never launch a channel or cluster around a generic seed keyword. Always drill down from Seed $\rightarrow$ Sub-Niche $\rightarrow$ Micro-Niche Entity Node.

### 1.1 The Seed-to-Micro-Niche Hierarchy
```
LEVEL 1: SEED KEYWORD (Mass market / High search / High saturation)
└── Example: "Inheritance Stories", "Karma Stories", "Stoic Wisdom"

LEVEL 2: SUB-NICHE CORRIDOR (Moderate search / Defensible intent)
└── Example: "Disinherited Son Stories", "Greedy Siblings Will", "Stoic Lessons on Betrayal"

LEVEL 3: MICRO-NICHE ENTITY NODE (Algorithmic Co-Recommendation Sweet Spot)
└── Example: "Disinherited for a rusted safe inheritance", "Monk boiling tea king paradox"
```

### 1.2 Incognito Wildcard (*) Autocomplete Sweep
Open YouTube in **Incognito Mode** (Location set to target market, e.g., US) and execute the 6 probe patterns:
1. `why the [core entity] *` $\rightarrow$ Validates causality and curiosity demand.
2. `the [entity A] and the *` $\rightarrow$ Validates classic archetype friction.
3. `story of a [character archetype] who *` $\rightarrow$ Validates moral/dramatic premise interest.
4. `[niche topic] that changed *` $\rightarrow$ Validates life-transformation search intent.
5. `never [action] a [entity] *` $\rightarrow$ Validates high-stakes warning demand.
6. `the [object] of the [entity] *` $\rightarrow$ Validates sacred prop and mystery interest.

### 1.3 Outlier Video Ratio Scan ($\ge 5\times$)
Identify 5 competitor channels in the proposed sub-niche:
- Filter channels with $< 100\text{K}$ subscribers.
- Sort videos by **Most Popular**.
- Calculate **Outlier Ratio**:
  $$\text{Outlier Ratio} = \frac{\text{Video View Count}}{\text{Channel Subscriber Count}}$$
- **Validation Threshold**: If at least 2 channels under 50K subscribers have videos with a ratio $\ge 5.0$ (e.g. 30K sub channel hitting 250K+ views), the niche has **confirmed algorithmic demand** and open browse traffic pools.

### 1.4 The 5-Point Niche Viability Scorecard (0–100 pts)
Score the niche across 5 pillars (full rubric in [references/keyword-niche-discovery-matrix.md](references/keyword-niche-discovery-matrix.md)):
1. **Search & Autocomplete Velocity** (25 pts): $\ge 10$ autocomplete suggestions + stable/rising Google Trends.
2. **Outlier Proof on Small Channels** (25 pts): Outlier ratios $\ge 5\times$ on channels $<50\text{K}$ subs.
3. **Monetization & RPM Tier** (20 pts): High RPM (\$5–\$15+ for drama/storytelling, \$15–\$35 for finance/tech).
4. **Content Scalability & Evergreen Shelf-Life** (15 pts): Reusable assets, evergreen interest for 3+ years.
5. **Blue-Ocean Content Gap** (15 pts): Visible packaging, script, or pacing gap in incumbent competitors.

**Decision Gate**: Score $\ge 75/100$ is required to proceed.

## STEP 3 — PACKAGING VELOCITY & HIGH-CTR TITLE MATRIX

### 3.1 Mobile-First 50–65 Character Standard
- **Character Length**: Strictly **50 – 65 characters** (100% visible on mobile without truncation). Absolute ceiling: 70 chars.
- **Keyword & Shock Placement**: Within the **first 3 – 5 words**.
- **Psychological Trigger**: Acute Curiosity Gap or Extreme Moral Paradox. Zero plot spoilers.

### 3.2 The 3-Part High-CTR Mathematical Title Anatomy
$$\text{Title} = \underbrace{\text{[Part 1: Canonical Root Hook (First 3–5 Words)]}}_{\text{Stops scroll in 0.3s; indexes Knowledge Graph}} + \underbrace{\text{[Part 2: Cognitive Dissonance / Paradox]}}_{\text{Two clashing realities create acute tension}} + \underbrace{\text{[Part 3: Unresolved Cliffhanger]}}_{\text{Forces involuntary click to resolve}}$$

### 3.3 The Packaging Synergy Law (Bình Thông Nhau)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **TITLE AND THUMBNAIL MUST NEVER DUPLICATE INFORMATION.**
> - **Title**: Delivers the **Logic Gap & Context** (high-stakes premise, numbers, unresolved conflict).
> - **Thumbnail**: Delivers the **Crime Scene of Injustice & Stark Adversity** (emotional shock, power dynamic, physical humiliation).
> - **Combined Effect**: Together they form an unanswered question that compels an instant click.

### 3.4 🎭 Mandatory Narrative Point of View (POV) Invariance Standard
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ABSOLUTE POV INVARIANCE BETWEEN STORY TITLE, AUDIO PROSE, AND ALL SEO ASSETS:**
> - **First-Person Stories (Protagonist as 'I')**:
>   * Story title and ALL 4 SEO title options MUST use First-Person (`I / My / Me`). Strictly FORBIDDEN to use Third-Person pronouns (`He / His / The Son`).
>   * Audio narration prose and Description Tier 1 Hook must be strictly First-Person.
> - **Third-Person Stories (Omniscient / Limited)**:
>   * Story title and all SEO titles MUST use Third-Person (`He / She / They / The Son`). Strictly FORBIDDEN to use First-Person confessional pronouns (`I / My / Me`) outside direct dialogue.
> - **Zero Pronoun Schizophrenia**: Mixing POV across titles and audio causes immediate disorientation, shattering 0–30s retention.

## STEP 4 — HIGH-CTR THUMBNAIL PACKAGING (THE 4 SOVEREIGN PILLARS)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **PERMANENT DOCTRINE: THE THUMBNAIL IS COMMERCIAL PACKAGING, NOT A PASSIVE SCENE.**
> Passive poses and flat drama (characters merely standing, crying, or arguing) die at CTR 1.2%.
> Every thumbnail prompt produced MUST actively enforce the **4 Sovereign Pillars**:

### 4.1 Pillar 1: Suspended Catastrophe in Mid-Motion (1/1000s Physical Rupture)
* **Strict Anti-Static Mandate**: Zero generic poses. Never capture characters merely looking, talking, or standing still.
* **1/1000th Second Catastrophe**: Capture the exact physical rupture frozen in mid-motion:
  * Crystal wine glass tilting mid-air, red wine splashing over legal documents.
  * Freshwater pearl necklace snapped, beads suspended floating across mahogany tables.
  * Crimson court summons slammed hard onto polished wood, dust and paper fibers bursting upward.
  * Arrogant billionaire on his knees in mud, soaked debt notices floating across rain puddles.
  * Ancient carved beam slamming down, cracking a fraudulent clay debt ledger in two.

### 4.2 Pillar 2: Extreme Duality & Somatic Delta (Split Reality & Ego Death)
* **Split-Reality Architecture**:
  * **Side A (Humble Underdog $\rightarrow$ Supreme Calm & Triumph)**: Worn rustic clothing, unshakeable serene calm, subtle 2mm micro-smile of vindication, firmly gripping the genuine seal, key, or signed receipt.
  * **Side B (Corrupt Elite $\rightarrow$ Bodily Ego Death)**: Bespoke attire, pupils dilated exposing sclera, cold sweat streaming down temples, throat tendons bulging, mouth agape mid-gasp, knees buckling.
* **Maximum Emotional Delta**: Communicates in 0.3s that the scales of justice violently flipped 180 degrees in a single fraction of a second.

### 4.3 Pillar 3: Embedded 3D Cinematic Text Treatment in Prompts (Zero Delegation)
* **Never defer to Canva or Photoshop**: AI engines (Ideogram 2.0, Midjourney v6.1, Flux Pro 1.1) render pristine typography when prompted with exact structural syntax.
* **The 3 Typographical Components**:
  1. **3D Hero Title**: Volumetric 3D beveled & embossed letters in glossy Ruby Red or weathered cracked Gold, sharp geometric outer black stroke, hard 0px-blur drop shadow.
  2. **Conflict Tagline Banner**: Rested horizontally on an energetic distressed acrylic brush-stroke banner in deep crimson or charcoal grunge.
  3. **Cognitive Gut-Punch Tagline**: Short (3–8 words), capitalized, zero punctuation, exposing core paradox (e.g., `"THEY TOOK THE ESTATE. THE DEBT TOOK THEM."`).

### 4.4 Pillar 4: Mobile Squint Test & Safe Zone Discipline
* **50%–60% Canvas Dominance**: Primary expressive face or weaponized prop MUST occupy 50%–60% of canvas height for instant readability on 5-inch phone screens.
* **Text Placement**: Locked at Lower-Center (offset clear of the bottom-right corner) or Left Column.
* **100% Clearance of Bottom-Right 20% (Danger Zone)**: Strictly zero text, zero faces, and zero critical props in the lower-right 20% where YouTube displays video timestamps (e.g. `54:20`).
* **Dual-Spectrum Chiaroscuro**: Clash between warm golden 2700K key light (on truth/protagonist) and cold slate-blue 6000K edge light (on collapsing antagonist & shadow void).

### 4.5 The 4 Standard Thumbnail Deliverable Variants
1. **Thumbnail A — SUSPENDED CRIME / INJUSTICE IN PROGRESS (Moral Outrage Hook)**: Active crime or humiliation mid-shatter.
2. **Thumbnail B — SPLIT-REALITY FORENSIC CHECKMATE (Extreme Duality & Proof Revelation)**: Physical match or unmasking where the genuine seal exposes fraud.
