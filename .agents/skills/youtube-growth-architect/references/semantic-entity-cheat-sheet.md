# Semantic Entity Cheat Sheet & Knowledge Graph Taxonomy (2026)

## Executive Overview

YouTube's Natural Language Processing (NLP) models index content through interconnected **Entity Graphs** rather than isolated keywords. An entity is a distinct, recognized concept (Person, Object, Location, Moral Concept, Action) that the algorithm uses to understand topic authority and determine which videos belong together in a **Suggested Binge-Loop co-recommendation shelf**.

This cheat sheet serves as a rapid-lookup catalog of validated entity primitives, relationship triples, bridge pairings, and saturation replacement protocols.

---

## 1. The 4 Core Entity Types & Emotional Archetypes

| Entity Category | Structural Role in Story | Production Examples | Algorithmic & Emotional Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master / Authority Figure** | Moral compass, wisdom anchor, test proctor | Zen Master, Blind Sage, Dying Tycoon, Silent Blacksmith, Elder Monk | Anchor entity; grounds the video in an established wisdom/spiritual Knowledge Graph. |
| **Challenger / Antagonist** | Conflict igniter, ego symbol, persecutor | Arrogant King, Greedy Eldest Son, Ruthless Landlord, Smug Heir, Fierce General | Drives early moral outrage; provokes involuntary click to see retribution. |
| **Vulnerable Innocent / Mirror** | Adversity carrier, catalyst of empathy | Starving Beggar, Disowned Daughter, Widowed Mother, Orphaned Apprentice | Triggers viewer empathy and protective instinct. |
| **Bystander / Greek Chorus** | Societal judgment, gossip amplifier | Village Merchant, Court Attendant, Dinner Guests, Relatives | Validates viewer's moral verdict during the climax. |

## 2. Symbolic Objects Taxonomy (Physical Anchor Props)

Symbolic objects are physical items that carry immense narrative and emotional weight. In YouTube packaging, the object anchors both the title and the thumbnail visual.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       SYMBOLIC OBJECT TAXONOMY                         │
├───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ HOT / VOLATILE OBJECTS    │ Boiling Tea, Poisoned Goblet, Burning Will,│

├───────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────┤

└───────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Karmic Actions & Consequence Matrix

Actions must be visually and morally polarizing. They form the kinetic bridge of the Entity Triple.

| Action Type | Examples | Emotional Function & Click Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Aggressive Provocation** | Tearing the will into pieces, spitting on the floor, pouring boiling water on hand, kicking the beggar's bowl | Maximum Moral Outrage (Rank 1 click driver); demands immediate justice. |
| **Subversive Stoic Silence** | Smiling while insulted, bowing to a tyrant, drinking cold water, walking away with empty hands | Disbelief & Cognitive Paradox; viewer must know: *"Why didn't they fight back?"* |
| **The Unintended Exposure** | Unlocking the rusted safe to find sawdust, opening the mother's letter at the bank, finding old receipts | Anagnorisis & Ego Death; sudden collapse of wrongful arrogance. |
| **Restorative Reconciliation** | Returning the stolen deed, kneeling at the graveyard, splitting the loaf of bread in silence | Emotional Catharsis & Share Trigger; leaves viewer uplifted. |

## 5. Adjacent Entity Substitution (Anti-Saturation Protocol)

When a core entity becomes overused across competing channels (dropping CTR $>30\%$ or falling out of YouTube autocomplete top-5), swap it with an **Adjacent Entity** from the same semantic family:

```
SATURATED ENTITY NODE          →    ADJACENT BLUE-OCEAN REPLACEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Zen Master"                   →    "The Blind Sage" / "The Dying Hermit"
"Boiling Tea"                  →    "Cold River Water" / "A Cracked Teacup"
"Arrogant King"                →    "Corrupt Magistrate" / "Smug Silk Merchant"
"Inheritance Money"            →    "Father's Rusted Safe" / "The Missing Deed"
"Revenge"                      →    "Silent Accountability" / "The Public Audit"
"Beggar"                       →    "Forgotten Blacksmith" / "Wounded Veteran"
```

```

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Buddhist Parable      +   Modern Psychology       =   "Why The Master Refused To Forgive: The 1 Trap Of False Peace" (61 chars)
```
